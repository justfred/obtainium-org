Commute Fuel Cost & Vehicle Replacement Analysis

Date: May 2026
Context: 2010 Lexus GX at 216K miles, evaluating replacement options

1. Current Situation

  • Vehicle: 2010 Lexus GX, 216K miles
  • Fuel: Premium, ~$6.50/gallon (CA, Iran war spike)
  • Efficiency: 15 mpg
  • Commute: 30 miles each way, 60 miles/day, ~250 days/year

Rule of thumb: fuel price per gallon ≈ annual cost in dollars — works when miles/year ÷ mpg ≈ 1,000 gallons. Holds exactly here: 15,000 mi ÷ 15 mpg = 1,000 gal × $6.50 = $6,500/year.

2. Recommendation

Keep the GX. Buy a RAV4 PHEV as the daily commuter.

  • GX handles off-road and towing (rarely needed but important)
  • RAV4 PHEV covers the commute near-electrically
  • 52-mile EV range covers each 30-mile leg fully on electric
  • With home charge + subsidized work charge: commute runs entirely electric
  • Avoids two-car inconvenience while capturing most EV efficiency gains

3. Detail

Why the GX stays

  • 216K miles = replacement is rational, but GX is body-on-frame with real off-road capability (locking rear diff, low range, KDSS)
  • Off-road camping and 2K towing are occasional but important use cases
  • No crossover/unibody replaces this without significant capability loss

RAV4 PHEV commute math

  • EV range: 52 miles (SE/XSE trim)
  • Commute: 30 miles each way — fits within EV range each leg
  • Home charge overnight (120V sufficient, ~12 hrs): covers morning leg
  • Work charge (subsidized ~$0.15/kWh): covers evening leg

Payback: PHEV premium over Hybrid

  • Price delta: ~$8,500
  • Annual savings delta: $5,447 − $2,093 = $3,354/yr
  • Payback: ~2.5 years — especially strong given subsidized work charging

Home charger

Probably not needed. 120V overnight is sufficient for 52-mile battery. Saves ~$1,200 install cost.

CA electricity rate (May 2026)

~$0.34/kWh residential. Work charger assumed ~$0.15/kWh (subsidized).

Gas price note

Current CA premium prices elevated by 2026 Iran war / Strait of Hormuz disruption. Baseline CA premium was ~$5.50 pre-conflict. Analysis holds at lower prices; savings shrink slightly.

If replacing the GX for capability

  • 4Runner Hybrid is the rational call — same i-FORCE MAX powertrain as Land Cruiser, 23 mpg, 6,000 lb tow, $14K less
  • Land Cruiser is the fun/prestige call — justified if you want it, hard to justify on economics alone
