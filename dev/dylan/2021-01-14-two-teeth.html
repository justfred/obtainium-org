Two Teeth

January 2021 · Age 7

Dylan turned seven on January 9, 2021. A few days later, something big happened to his smile.

On Wednesday, January 13, Dylan had both of his two front teeth pulled. That is the top middle two, the ones everybody sees first when you grin. Papa wrote it in his journal that day: “Dylan’s two front teeth pulled.”

Losing one tooth is a big deal. Losing both front teeth on the same day is a really big deal. When it was over, Dylan had a new gap right in the middle of his smile.

Now, a lost tooth means one thing: the tooth fairy. Everybody knows how it works. You give up a tooth, and the tooth fairy leaves you something in its place.

The next day, Thursday, January 14, Papa wrote one more line in his journal: “Tooth fairy five dollars.”

So the tooth fairy came, and she did not forget. Dylan had given up two teeth, and the tooth fairy left five dollars.

Five dollars is a good amount for a seven-year-old. It is more than a few candy bars. It is enough to feel like a real prize.

Papa also noted that this was the same week as his work’s big release, the one called R13. It was a busy week for Papa. But he still stopped to write down the teeth and the tooth fairy. Those were the things he wanted to remember.

Dylan’s grown-up teeth would come in later, to fill that gap. But for a little while, Dylan had a brand new smile, with a space in the middle of it, and five dollars to go with it.

That is the story of the two teeth.
