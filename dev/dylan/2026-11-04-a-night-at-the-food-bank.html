A Night at the Food Bank

November 4, 2026 · Age 12

On Wednesday, November 4, 2026, Papa had a chance to do something good with Dylan. A food bank needed helpers that evening, starting around five o’clock. Papa wrote in his journal that it was awesome that the two of them could go together.

A food bank is a place that gives food to people who need it. Before the food can go out, someone has to get it ready. That night, the job was packing lemons.

The helpers filled bags with lemons, one bag after another. Dylan was very focused. He kept his mind on the job, and he filled lots and lots of bags.

Papa wrote that evening that he was going to take Dylan to the food bank, and he did.
