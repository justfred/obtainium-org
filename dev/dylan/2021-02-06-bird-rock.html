Bird Rock

February 6, 2021 · Age 7

On Saturday, February 6, 2021, Papa took Dylan on a little trip. Dylan was seven.

First they went to Mission Bay Park. Mission Bay is a wide bay in San Diego.

After that, they went to a place called Bird Rock. Bird Rock is a spot along the coast in La Jolla, a part of San Diego. It is named for a big rock that sits just offshore, where birds like to gather. Papa’s journal says they went to the Bird Rock tide pools.

Tide pools are little pools of ocean water left behind in the rocks when the tide goes out.

Papa took a photo of Dylan that day. In the photo, Dylan is wearing a gray T-shirt with a picture on it, plaid shorts, and turquoise Crocs. He is stepping across the tide-pool rocks, and the rocks are covered in little hard shells called barnacles. Dylan is looking down at his feet to see where to step, and he is smiling. In his hand he is holding a little yellow toy.

That is what Dylan looked like at Bird Rock. He was careful, he was smiling, and he had a small yellow toy along for the trip.

In the notes Papa kept in 2021, Mission Bay and the Bird Rock tide pools were places the family went again and again.

So this was one Saturday out of many. It had a bay, and rocks, and tide pools, and a photo of a seven-year-old with a toy in his hand and a smile on his face.
