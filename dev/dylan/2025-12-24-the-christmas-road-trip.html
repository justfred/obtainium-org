The Christmas Road Trip

December 2025 · Age 11

Over Christmas, Dylan and Papa went on a road trip. Papa had the week off work, and he planned a long drive up through California and into Oregon. The trip lasted eight days. Papa drew the whole route on a map.

On Christmas Eve, they left home in the rain. There was a lot of rain around Los Angeles. But farther north, in the valley, the sky was clear. They stopped at a place called Harris Ranch. It had a big pool, and Dylan and Papa went for a swim. Then they had a fancy Christmas Eve dinner. The room had a couch that Dylan liked. Papa sent a picture of it to Mama, and Mama liked the couch too.

On Christmas Day, they drove to Sacramento. They walked around the State Capitol building and around Old Sacramento. Mama liked the bridge in the pictures. Then they went on to Chico, where Papa’s friend Felicia has a farm and cats. Dylan followed the cats around a little, but the cats and Dylan were not very interested in each other. They had Indian food for dinner. Dylan was a little tired of visiting that day.

Next, they drove north through the mountains. Papa wrote that they went through a snowstorm. They had pizza in Dunsmuir, and then they came to Ashland, in Oregon, to see another friend of Papa’s, Pamarie. Her house has deer, turkeys, squirrels and birds in the yard, and sometimes bears. She has lots of interesting coffee mugs. There were presents in Ashland, too.

On the way back south, Papa stopped at a store to buy a hat and gloves. They ate lunch near Mount Shasta and played in the snow. That night they stayed in a train hotel in Dunsmuir, where you eat dinner in a real dining car. Dylan liked the train place.

Then came the ocean. They went to Moonstone Beach at low tide, and there was so much sand. Dylan wore a cool hat. The next day, they went to Arcata, walked on the beach, and crossed some bridges. They saw a park in Ferndale, with a kitty, and a T-Rex. They saw the big trees, and tree carvings, and had lunch at the Eel River Cafe. Somewhere along the way, they also saw a goat.

Dylan kept a journal of the trip on his tablet. In the last entry he wrote the date, 12/31/25, Wednesday, Day 8. He wrote about the San Francisco Bay. He ended it with the words “back home to Mom!”
