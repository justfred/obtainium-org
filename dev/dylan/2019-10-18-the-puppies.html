The Puppies

October 2019 · Age 5

In the fall of 2019, three puppies came to stay at Dylan’s house. Dylan was five years old.

They were terriers. Terriers are small dogs with wiry fur. These three were only eight weeks old. That is very young, just barely old enough to be away from their mom.

The puppies were not staying forever. They were on loan. That means the family was taking care of them for a while, and then they would go back.

Papa wrote about them in his journal. He wrote what puppies do all day long. They pee. They poop. They eat. They sleep. They play. Then they do it all over again. That is a lot for three little puppies to fit into one day.

But puppies are also soft and warm. They have that puppy smell, which is one of the best smells there is.

And Dylan loved them. Papa wrote it down in just a few words: “Dylan loves them.”

There were three of them, and Dylan loved them all.
