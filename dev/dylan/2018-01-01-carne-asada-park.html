Carne Asada Park

January 1, 2018 · Age 3

It was New Year’s Day, 2018. Dylan was three years old. In just over a week, he would turn four.

Papa took Dylan to a park. Papa called it Carne Asada Park. Carne asada is grilled steak, and the name is a good clue about the place.

Dylan had just come back from a trip. He had been on an airplane. Papa wrote that Dylan smelled like an airplane. Papa had a funny way of writing it: “eau d’airplane.” That is a joke with French in it, like a fancy perfume.

Now he was out at a park, with a little break and some out-of-the-cage time, as Papa put it.

The park was full of kids. It was a little bit cold, because it was January. Lots of kids came anyway.

And Dylan went straight to the big slide.

He played on it. Papa wrote about it right then, at 4:39 in the afternoon. That is the whole story, and it is a good one. A boy, a big slide, and a lot of other kids on a chilly first day of the year.
