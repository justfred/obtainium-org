A Desert Weekend

February 2024 · Age 10

In February, Dylan had a long weekend off school. He had Friday, Monday and Tuesday off. Papa and Dylan decided to go on a short trip, just to go somewhere. Papa thought about Arizona, the Grand Canyon and Indian reservation areas. Dylan does not like national parks, so they set out for the desert instead.

The first stop was the Blythe Intaglios. These are huge pictures that were made on the desert ground long ago, and the Blythe ones are the only ones of their kind in North America, Papa said. Dylan stood at a chain-link fence there, in a blue shirt and jeans, with the desert, the mountains and the flat horizon behind him. Papa also flew his drone over the area to take pictures.

That night they stayed at the London Bridge Resort in Lake Havasu City, Arizona. Yes, there is a real London Bridge there. Dylan swam in the lake in the evening, in a red swim shirt and swim goggles. He waved at the camera. Later, he stood by the pool in a blue and white bathrobe with bear ears. Papa sent his mother a picture, and a picture of Dylan back in the pool.

The next night they were in Palm Springs, at a tiki hotel called the Caliente Tropics. A tiki hotel has carved statues, thatch roofs and tropical music. It is Papa’s favorite place to stay in Palm Springs, and it has a big warm pool. Dylan raised a hand in the air, excited, at the tiki bar. He posed next to a big tiki statue in a green light.

For dinner, Dylan and Papa went to an Italian restaurant with red-checked tablecloths. Dylan wore a green shirt with a tiki print. Papa took a picture of the two of them smiling. Dylan had pasta with sauce and fries and a can of Boylan soda.

That was the desert weekend. It ended on Tuesday, when Dylan had to be back.
