A Sunday Fun Day

June 2025 · Age 11

Dylan had a special kind of day with Papa. On the first Sunday of June, Papa wrote it at the top of his journal: “Sunday fun day with Dylan.”

It was a day with a lot of stops.

The first stop was a jumpy park. It was a new one, a trampoline park that Dylan and Papa had tried only a couple of weeks before. Dylan liked to jump, and Papa knew it. This time at the new park, something was different. Two weeks earlier, Papa had written, “Dylan had an easier time jumping off this time.” Papa noticed that jumping off was getting easier for Dylan.

Papa sent a few pictures to Mama from that visit. Mama looked at them and wrote back that it was a super high jump. She said she would be scared.

The next stop was lunch at Hamburger Hut. This was one of Dylan’s places. Papa had written that Dylan asked to go there about once a week.

Then they went to the slidey park. Papa wrote it down just like that, “slidey park.” After that came the spinny park, with things that go around and around. Dylan and Papa had been to the spinny park before, back in the winter, and Papa had planned to take Dylan to the slide park on a Saturday in February.

That night, Papa told his sister Amy about the day. He said that he and Dylan had been “eating and park-ing” all day.

Jumpy park, hamburgers, slidey park, spinny park. It was a good list for a Sunday.
