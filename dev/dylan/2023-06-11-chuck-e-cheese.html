Chuck E. Cheese

June 2023 · Age 9

It was a cold, rainy day in June. Papa and Dylan needed somewhere to go that was indoors.

Papa thought of two places. One was Dave and Buster’s. The other was Chuck E. Cheese. Papa picked Chuck E. Cheese.

Just a few days before, Papa and Dylan had been camping at Youtopia, a big festival out in the dry country. Dylan had danced to a Beatles cover band, pulled people onto the dance floor, and bounced on a couch. He had also watched sword fights at a place called Danger Camp. Now it was cold and wet and everything was different. Chuck E. Cheese was loud and bright and full of games, which was just right for a rainy day.

Chuck E. Cheese has a special rule. Nobody can leave unless they leave with the person they came in with. That meant Dylan was free to walk around the whole place on his own, and Papa did not have to follow him everywhere.

So Papa gave Dylan one hour to play video games, and Dylan went off to get his Chuck E. fix. That is what Papa called it in his journal. He wrote that it was good for Dylan to go and play on his own.

Papa found a corner and sat down with his little computer. He plugged it into a battery to see how long it would last. He was not sure the battery was charging it. Maybe it would just keep it running longer. It was a small experiment while Dylan played.

While they were there, Papa sent a message to Mama. He told her it was cold and rainy, so they had gone to Chuck E. Cheese. He thought about taking Dylan to see the Spider-Man movie afterward. Mama had been planning to take Dylan to that movie herself, because she wanted to see it too. Papa said okay, he could wait. Mama wrote that Chuck E. Cheese was a safe place for Dylan. Papa wrote back, “Yep.” Then he wrote, “Mario Brothers it is, then.”

Papa also had a plan for dinner. He thought they might go to Texas Roadhouse when they were finished.

Chuck E. Cheese was a place Dylan asked for. Later in 2023, Papa wrote to Mama that Dylan wanted to go there again, and they went to another one, in Temecula. Papa wrote to Aunt Amy that the two of them were at Chuck E. Cheese, and that one of them was enjoying it.

That rainy day was simple. It was cold outside, there was a place full of games, and Dylan got to go off and play for a whole hour.
