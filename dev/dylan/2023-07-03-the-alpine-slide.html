The Alpine Slide

July 2023 · Age 9

When Dylan was nine, he and Papa spent more than a week in Colorado. They flew to Denver on the 24th of June, rented a car, and drove a big loop through the mountains. They stayed with Grammy and Gramps, and Dylan went with them to Cripple Creek and rode a train. They crossed the Royal Gorge Bridge. They swam in hot springs pools in Pagosa Springs and in Glenwood Springs. They floated sticks in a creek in Telluride. On the second of July they went to Black Canyon of the Gunnison, walked to some waterfalls, and had lunch at a pub nearby. Papa tried to keep each day’s drive short, but there was a lot of driving anyway.

Near the end of the trip, they went to an alpine slide. Dylan and Papa each rode in their own sled, down a long concrete track all the way down the mountain. It was fast.

Papa paid for four rides. It cost 120 dollars, which is a lot for four rides. Papa wrote about it that night. He said Dylan loved the alpine slide, and that he was glad Dylan did, because of what it cost.

The morning they left was not an easy one for Papa. He had hardly slept the night before, and he had a headache. He also tripped and fell down a step that morning. He was not badly hurt, but about a week later his ankle was sore enough that he needed crutches for a while.

That afternoon, they flew home from Denver to San Diego. The plane was high and hot and bumpy. Papa looked down at the land they had driven through. He thought they were flying over the Colorado River, in Utah. He wished he had remembered to download an app that tells you what you are flying over. He was usually good at working it out for himself.

Dylan played Minecraft for most of the flight. He had played a lot of Minecraft that week. Papa wrote that Dylan had looked out the windows a little, too. On this flight, Dylan was killing polar bears in the game. Papa wrote that Dylan was a good passenger, even with the polar bear killing.

When they got home, Papa sent Grandma Elise a link to his photos of the trip, and she said there were lots of great ones. He also had the paper diary that Grammy had given Dylan. Papa had not finished it, but he had written a few more days in it. He planned to use the photos to fill in the rest.

That was how the Colorado trip ended. There were mountains, hot springs, a train, a creek, a gondola, and four rides on the alpine slide.
