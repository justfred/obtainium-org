Mission Bay

January 9, 2021 · Age 7

January 9 is Dylan’s birthday. In 2021, he turned seven.

It was a Saturday. Papa wrote in his journal that day: “Dylan’s birthday, Mission Bay.”

Mission Bay is a big, wide bay in San Diego, with water, beaches, and grassy parks along the edge. The family went there to celebrate. It was a good place for a birthday party, with lots of room for people to be together.

Dylan did not celebrate alone. Many people from his family came to Mission Bay that day.

Auntie Amy came. Amy is Papa’s sister. Her daughters came too, Dylan’s cousins Chloe and Lydia.

Two more people came, too: Cindy and Mark. Cindy is Mama’s mother, so she is Dylan’s grandmother. Mark is Cindy’s husband.

That makes a lot of people for one small boy’s birthday. Dylan had his aunt, his two cousins, and his grandmother and her husband, all together at the water with Papa.

Seven is a good age to be. By seven, a kid can run fast, tell what he likes, and pick his favorite people out of a crowd. Dylan had a whole group of favorites there on the shore.

Papa did not write down what they ate, or what presents Dylan opened, or what the bay looked like that day. He only wrote down who was there and where. But Papa kept that line, and now it is part of the story of Dylan growing up.

The day was a Saturday. The place was Mission Bay. The people were the ones who love Dylan. And Dylan was seven.

That is the story of the birthday.
