Kindergarten

August–September 2019 · Age 5

In August 2019, Dylan started kindergarten. He was five years old.

Kindergarten is the first year of real school. It was a new start for Dylan, and a new schedule for the whole house.

School starts early in the morning. To get Dylan ready and out the door on time, everyone in the house had to be up at six o’clock. That is very early, especially in the summer. Papa had to get up when he hadn’t before.

So every school day began the same way. The alarm went off. Dylan got up and got dressed. He had breakfast. Then he went to school.

Papa wrote about it in his journal about two weeks after school started. He said it was a busy and crazy time. A lot was going on in the family, and Papa was getting ready for a trip to a festival in the desert called Wasteland Weekend, where he and his friends were putting together costumes. Becke was switching jobs. Papa’s own work contract was almost over. But in the middle of all of that, one thing was steady. Dylan had started kindergarten, and they had to get up at six to get him ready.

That is how kindergarten started for Dylan. Alarm, breakfast, out the door. Every day.
