Pasquale

Spring 2013 · Before Dylan was born

Before Dylan was born, a parrot came to live at the house. The parrot’s name was Pasquale.

Pasquale was a yellow-naped Amazon parrot. That is a green parrot with a patch of yellow on the back of its neck. Pasquale was fifteen years old, which is a lot of years for most animals, but parrots can live a very long time.

Pasquale came from the neighbors next door. They had kept Pasquale for a while, and now they were passing Pasquale on to Papa and Mama. Mama worked at a bird hospital, so she knew a lot about birds and had help learning more. Papa and Mama said they were about to become parrot parents.

At the neighbors’ house, Pasquale had been living outside, in a homemade aviary. An aviary is a big cage that a bird can walk and climb around in. So Pasquale was already used to having a lot of room. Papa and Mama had to figure out how to make a new space with some shade. It also needed to keep Pasquale safe from animals, small ones and big ones.

First they found a used aviary from a bird breeder. It was a big box made of chain-link fence. They went and picked it up. Papa’s car was a Land Rover, and it carried five big sections of chain-link on its roof. Inside it carried about ten long, thick deck boards, some other lumber, and half a dozen concrete blocks. Those were for a small raised deck in the yard to set the aviary on.

Papa also built a roof for the parrot cage, and a rain roof for the back porch. He wrote about that busy weekend of building.

They put the aviary among the trees in the yard. They hoped it would be far enough from the house so the noise would not bother the person who rented a part of the property.

They also got a secondhand cage for inside the house. That way Pasquale could get used to the cats and the dog. Pasquale could talk, had been hand-fed, and also made loud beeping noises.

Pasquale liked loquats. That is a small orange fruit that grows on trees.

When Dylan came along, Pasquale was already there.
