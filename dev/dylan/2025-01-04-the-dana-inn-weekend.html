The Dana Inn Weekend

January 2025 · Age 10

In early January, just a few days before Dylan’s eleventh birthday, Papa booked a hotel for the weekend. It was for just the two of them, Dylan and Papa.

The hotel was the Dana Inn, on Mission Bay in San Diego. Papa picked it because it had a warm pool and a buffet breakfast. Those are two very good things for a weekend away.

Papa had a plan. They would eat dinner somewhere nearby. Maybe they would ride bikes. Maybe they would go to the roller coaster. Papa thought the roller coaster might be a surprise, because Dylan wasn’t expecting it.

Getting there was easy. Dylan’s bike and Papa’s folding bike both fit inside the car, with room left over for Dylan.

Then came a nice surprise. When they got to the hotel, they were upgraded to a room with a view of the bay. Papa wrote that it was lovely. Papa liked the Dana Inn so much that he wrote he could just stay there.

For dinner, they went to a place called Hideaway Mission Beach. It was busy and a little chaotic, but people say the food there is very good. The waiter was named Mario. Papa wrote that dinner was good.

Then there was the pool. Papa wrote it plainly: “Pool was warm enough for Dylan.” A warm pool in January is a fine thing to have.

After dinner, Papa thought they might walk around a little.

There was still more to look forward to. The hotel had a good breakfast, and there were the bikes, the park, and maybe that roller coaster.

A weekend away with Papa, a bay-view room, a warm pool, and a bike for Dylan in the car. Not a bad start to the year.
