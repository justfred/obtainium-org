The Trampoline Park

September 2023 · Age 9

Dylan liked to jump. Papa knew that, and so did everyone who knew Dylan. Once, in February, Papa asked Mama if it was okay to take Dylan to the trampoline park right then. Mama wrote back, “Dang, he has a lot of energy.” Then she said sure.

On the days when Dylan was with Papa, they had a few places they liked to go. There was the beach, the park in Encinitas, and the jumpy park. The jumpy park was Altitude, a big trampoline park. Dylan had a membership there. That meant he and Papa could go whenever Dylan wanted to, and Dylan often wanted to.

One Saturday in September, they went again. They had been there two weeks before, and they would be back a few weeks after that. On this Saturday, Papa sat down and wrote in his journal while Dylan played. He wrote that Dylan could spend hours on the high dive.

The high dive was Dylan’s favorite thing at Altitude. It was better than the regular trampolines. It was better than the foam pit and the dodgeball court. To do it, Dylan climbed up to a high platform, and then he jumped off into a giant pit full of soft foam blocks. Then he climbed out of the foam, went back up to the platform, and did it again.

Most kids might do that once or twice. Dylan could keep going for hours. Papa sat on the side and watched. After a while he lost count of how many jumps there had been. It did not really matter. Every time Dylan jumped, he grinned.

Papa was glad Dylan had a place like that. He wrote that it was good for Dylan to get outside time with other kids. There were other kids around, and sometimes they were from Dylan’s school. A few weeks later, Papa wrote that there were lots of kids from Dylan’s school at the bouncy park that day.

Papa also wrote about something that had happened a few days before this Saturday. Mama had taken Dylan to Altitude, and Papa said Dylan had a good time with her. Dylan had been learning to jump over bolsters. A bolster is a long, round cushion, a bit like a big pillow shaped like a log. Papa wrote that Dylan had a good interaction while he was learning it.

Papa did not know whether Dylan was trying to do the high dive perfectly or whether he just liked that one second in the air before the foam caught him. Maybe it was both.

On that Saturday, Dylan did the high dive again and again.
