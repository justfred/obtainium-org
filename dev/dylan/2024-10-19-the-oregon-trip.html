The Oregon Trip

October 2024 · Age 10

In October, when Dylan was ten, the whole family went to Oregon. Papa, Mama, Aunt Amy and Dylan flew to Portland on a Saturday evening. They had dinner at Red Robin near the airport and slept at a hotel by the airport. The trip lasted eight days.

On Sunday, they swam at the hotel and had biscuits. They went to Washington Park and the Rose Garden. That evening they had dinner with friends, the Cubbages, and then drove south to the town of Salem.

On Monday, they went to Silver Falls and hiked about five miles. Then they spent the afternoon and evening at Belknap Hot Springs, in warm water.

Then they drove down to the Grants Pass area. They visited a farm, a bakery and an alpaca farm. In the afternoon, they went to a place called the Out ‘n’ About Treesort, which is a place with sleeping rooms up in the trees. Papa wrote down a few things people said that day. Someone said, “It doesn’t look like it’s going to rain today.” Someone else said, “Maybe.” Someone asked, “What’s an aorta?” And someone answered, “It’s a part of your heart.”

On Wednesday, there was a zip line. There is a photo of Dylan from that day in a full climbing harness and a helmet, wearing a blue rain jacket, looking back over his shoulder. After the zip line, they had lunch at Kaleidoscope Pizza.

On Thursday, they drove the Oregon coast. They stopped at Whaleshead Beach and at a place in Coos Bay called 7 Devils. They stayed that night at an inn in Florence. In the night there was a fire alarm at the hotel.

On Friday, they drove north along the coast. They visited the Heceta Head Lighthouse and went tidepooling. They ate lunch in Yachats at Luna Sea Fish House. Then they went through Newport and came to Cannon Beach, where their hotel was the Inn at Haystack Rock.

On Saturday morning, they stayed in Cannon Beach. Then they drove to the Tillamook Creamery. After that, they went home.
