Dora at the Beach

2012–2013 · Before Dylan was born

Before Dylan was born, Dora and Papa went on a lot of walks. Many of them were near the ocean.

Dora was a small dog, about seventeen pounds. But she loved to move. Papa liked to take her places where she could stretch her legs.

One place was Oceanside Harbor. It is a harbor full of boats. On one Sunday in December 2012, Papa took Dora there for a long, good walk around the whole place. Then they walked out along the breakwater. A breakwater is a long wall of big rocks that sticks out into the water. It keeps the waves from crashing into the boats.

Another place was Carlsbad. Papa wrote about taking Dora for a walk there in June 2013. Dogs are not allowed on that beach, so they did not walk on the sand. They walked near it.

Her favorite place might have been Fiesta Island. Fiesta Island is in Mission Bay, in San Diego. Papa wrote that when they went there, Dora got to run around and chase other dogs. That is one of the things dogs like best. There is room to run, and plenty of other dogs to run after. Papa wrote about walking “the dogs” there in February 2013, and about a quick beach walk where Dora ran and chased dogs.

Papa liked those walks too. One day at Fiesta Island, in the spring of 2013, Papa was walking Dora by the water. He wrote that he thought his life was perfect. He wrote that it felt like a perfect moment. He thought that if the next day was his last, he would have had a really good life. He said that getting Dora had been great, and that having her meant a lot to him.

That is a nice thing to think while walking a dog. It does not mean everything in life was easy that day. It means that for a little while, Papa was exactly where he wanted to be. He was by the water, on a nice day, with a good dog.

Dora was a good dog. Dylan came along a little later, and Dora was there for him too.
