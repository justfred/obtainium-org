How Dora Got Here

February 2010 · Four years before Dylan was born

Before Dylan was born, before Papa and Mama had any kids at all, they were looking for a dog. Their first dog. They did not have one yet, and they wanted one very much.

Dora’s story starts a long way from the ocean. She started out in El Centro, a flat, hot city in the desert near the Mexican border. That is the same town where Grandma Elise and Grandpa Phil lived. Dora was a stray. That means she did not have a family. She was a dog on her own, and nobody knew exactly when she was born. The best guess was the fall of 2008, so she was a young dog.

Somehow Dora made her way from the desert to a shelter in Encinitas. A shelter is a place where dogs and cats wait for a new home. Encinitas is a beach town in San Diego County. It has more trees than El Centro, and a lot more fog, and the ocean is close by.

Mama worked at that shelter. Every day she was around dogs who needed homes. One of them was Dora.

Mama found her there. When Mama came home, she told Papa about the dog. There is a video from the time Dora joined the family. On it, you can hear Mama’s voice. She says, “Come here, Dora. You are so precious.” She says, “We are looking for our first dog.”

That was February 18, 2010. It is the day Dora came home with Papa and Mama.

Dora did not stay a stray. From then on, she had a family and a house. She had Papa and Mama, and she had the house’s cats and, later, a parrot named Pasquale. She had long walks and many trips to the beach.

Dora was a small dog. When she was about four years old, she weighed around seventeen pounds. Small, but she was a lot of dog.

A little less than four years after that day, Dylan was born. Dora was already part of the family when he came home. She had been there first.

So that is how Dora got here. She came from the desert, to a shelter by the sea, and then to a home where somebody had been looking for exactly a dog like her.
