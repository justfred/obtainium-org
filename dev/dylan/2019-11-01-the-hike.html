The Hike

November 2019 · Dylan, age 5 · Dora, age 11

In the fall of 2019, Dylan was five years old. He had a dog named Dora, who lived with the family. Dora had joined the family four years before Dylan was born, so she was a grown-up dog, and by this time she was getting older.

One day in November, Papa decided to take Dora on a hike. This was a hike for just the two of them. Dylan was not on this one.

They walked for about two hours. That is a long time to walk. Papa wrote it down in his journal that night. He said it was more walking than either of them had done in a long time.

Think about that. Papa had not walked that much in a long time. And Dora had not walked that much in a long time, either. Two hours is a lot of steps for a man, and a lot of steps for a dog with older legs.

But they did it. Papa and Dora walked the whole way together, side by side, and they made it home.

Dora was a good walking buddy. She liked beaches and she liked walks, and she had walked with Papa for many years already. She kept living with the family for more than four years after that hike.

This was just one ordinary day in the long friendship of one man and one dog. Nothing big happened on the hike. Nobody got lost. Nobody found a treasure. They just walked for two hours, and then they went home.

Sometimes that is a good enough story.
