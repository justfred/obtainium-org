Papa

2026 · Age 12

Every day, Papa asks Dylan about his day. On weekends he asks twice.

He asks what Dylan did at school. He asks what sports he played and what he had for lunch. He asks what songs he is singing. He asks who his favorite friends and teachers were, and who dropped him off, and who picked him up. Papa asks about math and reading, too. These days it is mostly math.

Some of Papa’s questions are silly. He might ask what Dylan thinks about the weather, or whether spaghetti is better than pizza, or the other way around.

Dylan knows when a question is silly. When Papa asks one, Dylan tilts his head to one side and says one word: “Papa!”

That one word says a lot. It says he knows Papa is being ridiculous. It says he finds this both tiresome and funny. It says he has no plan to stop the conversation. Often he follows it with the real answer. Sometimes the real answer is “I don’t know.” Papa asks anyway.

Dylan has a good sense of humor, especially when Papa teases him a little or makes a joke.

At night, Dylan still wants a story before bed. He has wanted this for as long as Papa can remember. He likes it best when Papa reads out loud, though he is fine with a story from memory too. Papa reads to him every night. Stick Fluffy, his toy, is usually there for the stories. Dylan brings Stick Fluffy with him when he goes places, the way he always has.

Dylan and Papa also build with Legos, and they talk about their day and their dreams.

After school and on weekends, Dylan dances. He has strong opinions about music, especially a group called KPOP Demon Hunters. He doesn’t just listen to the music. He moves to it, with full commitment. The dancing is serious, and it is also joyful.

Dylan is twelve now. He is getting taller all the time. He is turning into a teenager. He knows when a question is silly, and he knows when a story is a good one. He knows which toys come along and which stay home.

Papa still asks the questions anyway.
