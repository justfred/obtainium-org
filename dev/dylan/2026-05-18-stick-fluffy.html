Stick Fluffy

May 2026 · Age 12

When Dylan was twelve, he still carried Stick Fluffy with him wherever he went.

Stick Fluffy was originally a cat toy. When the cats passed away and their things were being given away, Dylan dug Stick Fluffy out of the giveaway pile and kept her. The name says what she looks like.

There was a whole box of other toys too. Raccoon was in it, and the rest. Most of them just rode along in the box. Stick Fluffy was the one Dylan really wanted with him.

Dylan still sleeps happily on a floor pad. At night he still wants a story before bed. He likes it best when Papa reads it out loud.

Dylan was growing up in other ways. He was getting taller all the time, and he was nice and slim. His hair still grew only in a few small patches. He loved babies and small children. He did amazingly well on the Scout swim test, which Scouts have to pass to go rafting. He was learning about hugging: it is okay to hug people after you know their name.

He knew what he liked to do. Often that was Minecraft, or silly YouTube videos, or Goat Simulator. He had friends who came for respite visits, and he liked them. He also loved Sally’s fire poi, a kind of spinning prop on ropes. He hasn’t played with them since Sally borrowed them back.

And Dylan loved to dance. His favorite was KPOP Demon Hunters. He danced to it with full commitment.

Every day, Papa asked him the same questions. How was school? What sports did you play? What was for lunch? What are you singing? Who were your favorite friends and teachers? Who dropped you off, and who picked you up? A lot of the time Dylan answered. Sometimes he said, “I don’t know.” Sometimes he tilted his head and said, “Papa!” in a way that means the question was too silly to take seriously. Papa asked anyway.
