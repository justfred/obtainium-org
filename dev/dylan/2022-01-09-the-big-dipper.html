The Big Dipper

January 2022 · Age 8

Dylan turned eight on January 9, 2022. That weekend, Papa wrote in his journal that they might go to Belmont Park, to the roller coaster and the rides. He wrote that it should be amusing enough.

They went. Belmont Park is at the beach in San Diego, and its big roller coaster is the Big Dipper.

Dylan rode the Big Dipper. He did not ride it just once. He rode it several times.

Notes for the storyteller

Known

Fred’s journal 2201j (Jan 2022): “This weekend is Dylan’s 8th birthday and I guess we might go to Belmont Park to the roller coaster and rides. Should be amusing enough.” (A plan only.)

Confirmed by Fred, 2026-11-09: they went, and Dylan rode “the big dipper roller coaster, several times.”

External fact (not from Fred): the roller coaster at Belmont Park is officially called the Giant Dipper; Fred calls it the Big Dipper.

People: Papa (Fred) and Dylan.

Not known: the exact day (the birthday weekend, probably Jan 8–9, 2022); whether Mama came; how many times he rode; what Dylan said or felt; what other rides or food. The earlier candidate list wrongly dated this to February.
