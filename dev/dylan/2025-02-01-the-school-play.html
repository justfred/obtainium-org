The School Play

January–February 2025 · Age 11

In the winter, when Dylan was eleven, his school put on a play. The play was Shrek. Dylan was in it.

Mama helped out, too. She was behind the scenes, helping with the costumes for the show.

There was a special performance on a Friday for the rest of the school. It started at 12:10 in the afternoon. Mama asked the teacher if Papa and Dylan’s cousin Lydia could come and watch. The teacher said yes. They had to check in at the school office and show their ID. Papa was not able to see the Saturday show. He was on a trip to San Francisco with Aunt Amy.

Then came the first show on Saturday. At the show, Dylan was not feeling very well. He had a fever a week earlier, and a lot of kids in his class had been sick. During the first show, Mama had to run out and buy some Tylenol.

But then something happened. Mama wrote to Papa that Dylan “pepped up on stage since he had people watching him.”

That night, everyone was tired. Mama said they were all exhausted, and they went to bed.

Papa was far away, but he sent a few photos to Dylan’s green iPad. He hoped the play had gone well.

The photos had to wait a little, because the green iPad was out of power. But Dylan made it to soccer, and he kicked the soccer balls right into the goalpost.

Being in a play takes a lot of time. Later, Mama wrote that Dylan had missed more than a month of ice hockey practice, because of the play and because he had been sick. Then it was back to ice hockey.
