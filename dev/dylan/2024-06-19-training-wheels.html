Training Wheels

May–June 2024 · Age 10

In the spring, when Dylan was ten, he took a bicycle class. At the end of the class there was a graduation. It was close to where Papa was working that week, and Papa planned to go and see Dylan graduate. After that, Papa was going home for the long Memorial Day weekend.

A few weeks later, Papa got a surprise. On the 19th of June, which is Juneteenth, he had the day off. He spent most of it with Dylan.

They went bike riding. Dylan’s bike had training wheels on it, and Dylan did a great job. Papa was proud of how well he rode.

Papa had a plan for what comes next. The training wheels do not have to stay down forever. They can be raised, just a little bit at a time. Each time they go up a little, Dylan has to balance a little more on his own. Papa knew it would take a lot of practice. He wrote that they just needed to raise the wheels a little at a time and practice a lot.

After the bike riding, they went to lunch. Dylan had his usual favorite, a rice and chicken bowl. For a while, Dylan had been asking for panda orange chicken every time. But that day he was not interested in it, so it was the rice and chicken bowl again.

Then they spent a while at the bouncy place. Later that day, Dylan went to horses with Mom.

Papa did get a little work done that morning, too. But mostly it was a day off, spent with Dylan.

That was a good day: a bike, training wheels, a bowl of rice and chicken, and a lot of bouncing.
