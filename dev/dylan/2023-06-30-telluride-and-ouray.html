Telluride and Ouray

June 2023 · Age 9

In late June, Papa and Dylan took a road trip through the mountains of Colorado. They had flown from San Diego to Denver and rented a car. The first days were spent with Grammy Cindy and Gramps Mark, and Dylan had a good day with them, including a ride on a train at Cripple Creek. Then Papa and Dylan drove on by themselves. Papa had planned a big loop. He tried to keep each day’s drive short, but it was still a lot of driving, and Papa said it was easier for him than for Dylan.

One day they stopped at the Royal Gorge Bridge, a very high bridge over a canyon. Papa wrote that Dylan liked it more than he did. Next they came to Pagosa Springs. The hot springs there have pools of different temperatures, and one was about 95 degrees. The pools sit right next to the San Juan River, which was much colder. Papa also saw a black bear near the road at Wolf Creek Pass. Dylan was asleep in the car and missed it.

From there they drove to Mesa Verde, and then on into the mountains toward Ouray. Papa said Dylan was cranky at Mesa Verde, because there had been too much driving. But when they got to Ouray, Papa sent a message that said the mountains were spectacular. People call Ouray the “Switzerland of America.” It is a small town with tall mountains standing all around it.

In Ouray, they stayed at a hotel with a big pool, a free breakfast, and hot tubs right inside the building. Dylan approved.

The next day, they went to Telluride. Telluride is another mountain town. It has a park in the middle of town, and a river runs right near it. They rode the free gondola up the mountain and back down. Papa sent Mama a photo with the words “Up and down.” Dylan loved the ride. Then they walked around town and stopped to talk to people and their dogs.

Near the park there was a tiny creek. Dylan found some sticks and leaves and floated them down the water. Down at the big stream, Papa noticed that the water was only about 36 degrees. Dogs were leaping in after balls and sticks, swimming out and coming back again. Papa liked watching them. So did Dylan.

Papa wrote down a list of animals they saw on the trip. It included a bear, a buffalo, a wild turkey, and a marmot.

At the end of the day, Papa wrote that Dylan liked floating sticks and leaves in the tiny creek near the park, and that he watched dogs swimming for balls in the river. That was the whole day. Two towns, a gondola, a creek, and some dogs.
