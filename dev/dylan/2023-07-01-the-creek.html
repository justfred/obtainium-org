The Creek

June 2023 · Age 9

In the mountain towns of Colorado, little creeks run almost everywhere. They are fed by melting snow from higher up, so the water is quick and cold and so clear that you can see every pebble on the bottom. It runs fast in the narrow parts and slows down where it spreads out.

Dylan and Papa were on a road trip through those mountains. They had been driving for days. There had been a hot springs pool, a big bridge over a canyon, and a black bear that Papa saw by the road while Dylan slept in the back seat. On this day they were in the town of Telluride. Near a park in the middle of town, there was a creek just like the ones in the stories. Dylan found it.

He was not doing anything complicated. He found some sticks and leaves and set them in the water at the top of a little current. Then he watched them travel. The current carried them off. So he got more sticks. He tried a bigger leaf. He tried a shorter stick. Dylan stood there for a long time, watching each one go, while Papa sat on a bench nearby and watched Dylan.

That day, a message went from Papa’s phone to Mama. It said, “Hi mom mom I found a stick.”

A little farther on there was a bigger stream, wide and fast. Papa noticed that the water was only about 36 degrees, so cold that his feet would go numb in about a minute. People had brought their dogs there. The dogs leaped in after balls and sticks, swam out into the current, and swam back, dripping and happy, over and over. Dylan watched the dogs the same way he had watched his sticks. He was interested, and he was in no hurry at all.

Papa liked the big stream best. He told Grandma Elise that he did. Then he wrote that Dylan liked floating leaves down the little one. They each had a favorite. Papa took lots of photos of the water that week. Later, Grandma Elise looked at them and said she loved all the flowing water.

That week had bigger things in it. There were hot springs, long drives through the mountains, a train ride with Grammy and Gramps, and the alpine slide that Dylan loved. But there was nothing big about this part. Dylan stood at the edge of a small creek in Colorado, trying out leaves and watching to see how far they would go.
