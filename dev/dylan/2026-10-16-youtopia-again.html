Youtopia, Again

October 2026 · Age 12

In October 2026, Dylan was twelve years old. Three years after his first Youtopia, he wanted to go again. Youtopia is the San Diego area’s own Burning Man event, a festival where people camp together, make art, and dance.

Papa’s back was still hurting. But Dylan wanted to go, so they went.

First they had to set up the tent. Then Papa found out he had been sent to the wrong place. He had camped on the spot where he had camped before, but it was now a space for people who live there, and it was not marked. So Papa took the tent down, moved it, and set it up again. Then they walked around, moved again, and had dinner.

After dinner, Dylan and Papa dressed warm. They walked around the festival at night, from about six o’clock until midnight. Papa brought a small chair to sit on.

Dylan loved dancing. He loved the games. And he loved hugging people, especially the greeters.

Late in the night, they ran into their friends Brady and Becca. The four of them went on a long walk around the top of camp. They saw lots of cool art and met some cool people. Papa wrote that they had good conversations.

Finally they went to bed, around 12:30. The tent was windy and flappy, and Papa did not sleep well.

The next day, more wind was coming. Papa took the tent down again and put it away. They went on a walk. There was not a lot to do during the day, so they decided to leave.

But they did not go straight home. Instead, they went to visit Grandma Elise. Dylan got to swim in her pool.

That was Youtopia, again. Papa wrote that they had a good day. Dylan wanted to go, and Dylan got to go.
