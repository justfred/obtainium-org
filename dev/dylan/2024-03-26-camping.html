Camping

March 2024 · Age 10

In March, Papa took Dylan camping. They went to the desert near Ocotillo, in California. It was a weekend trip, just the two of them.

The weather was not friendly. It was windy, and it rained. Neither of them got much sleep.

Papa never feels like cooking when he camps. That trip was no different. Papa also tends to wake up ready to leave, and that morning he did. After a windy, rainy night he was cold, tired, and a little stiff.

Dylan woke up happy.

The two of them did not go straight home. They drove a little of the De Anza trail, which runs through the desert near Ocotillo. For Papa it was a small adventure. Dylan took a nap while they drove.

Then it was time to head home. On the drive, Dylan said he would like to go camping every weekend, if he could.

Papa wrote that down.
