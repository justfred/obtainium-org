A Year of Sports

Composite of moments from fall 2024 to fall 2025 · Age 10 and 11

Dylan plays a lot of sports. If you wrote down all of them, you would need a long list.

There is soccer. In February of 2025, Dylan was at soccer and he kicked the balls right into the goalpost.

There is basketball. One Sunday in the fall of 2024, Dylan wanted to go to the beach, but Mama reminded him that he had basketball at 1 o’clock, and then a birthday party. Papa went to Dylan’s basketball finals in the first days of November. A year later, in September of 2025, the team took pictures at the start of practice. Dylan had a purple wrist sweatband for basketball. One day, a boy named Isaac was Dylan’s basketball buddy. Papa wrote to Mama that Dylan “did great dribbling.” Another day, Papa wrote that Dylan “had good basketball.” Dylan even went with Mama to see a real Clippers game in the new stadium in Oceanside.

There is ice hockey. In the fall of 2024, Mama took Dylan to Poway to check it out. A few weeks later, Mama went to Play It Again Sports to find hockey gear. Dylan’s group is called San Diego Special Hockey. Dylan changes in dressing room number one. In the spring of 2025, he went to hockey games in Irvine, and in the summer he started skating lessons, where kids learn to skate better and learn the game.

There is running. Papa wrote in his journal, “Dylan had a great day running.”

And there is swimming. In May of 2025, Papa wrote that Dylan “did great at swim.” The teacher said there were two or three lessons left, and gave Dylan two things to practice. For backstroke, keep the legs straight. For the crawl, use knife hands.

That is a lot of kicking, dribbling, skating, running and swimming. And Dylan keeps going.
