Dylan and the Water

Several years of swims, 2023 to 2026 · Age 12 (a composite of real days)

Dylan does lots of sports. He plays basketball with a team called the Shooting Stars. He plays ice hockey and soccer, and he runs. None of it is competitive. He is fit, and he is coordinated. But Papa says swimming is his best sport. He swims with a program called Waterworks Aquatics.

Dylan has been swimming for a long time. When he was nine, Papa wrote that he took Dylan to therapy, to school, and to swim, all in one day. Swimming was part of Dylan’s week.

Then there are the pools. One weekend in January, Papa and Dylan stayed at the Dana Inn on Mission Bay. Papa picked that hotel because it had a warm pool. The room looked out over the bay. Papa wrote it plainly: “Pool was warm enough for Dylan.” Dylan went swimming.

Another time, Papa and Dylan went to a get-together at a friend’s house. The friend’s name was Stefan. Stefan had a pool, and he had a daughter named Zoe. Papa wrote that Dylan loved the pool, and that he loved Zoe too.

Another time, Papa and Dylan went to Youtopia and camped in the wind. The tent was flappy. They did not stay long, and on the way home they stopped to visit Grandma. Grandma has a pool. Dylan got to swim in it.

Then there was the Scouts. Dylan joined the Boy Scouts this year. To go on the rafting trip, Scouts had to pass a swim test. Papa wrote that Dylan did amazingly well. Papa passed the test too.

In May, Dylan and Papa went with the Scouts to the Kern River for a rafting campout. Dylan paddled with the rest of the boat. He held on through the rapids. When the water got calm, Dylan jumped out of the raft and swam in the river. Papa said Dylan did great.

Dylan swam in a warm hotel pool, in friends’ pools, in Grandma’s pool, and in a river with his Scout troop. That is a lot of water.

Papa still asks him every day what sports he played. Sometimes Dylan tells him. Sometimes he says “I don’t know.” But when it is a day with water, Papa has a pretty good idea.

Notes for the storyteller

This is a composite. It strings together real swims from different dates, plus Fred’s own summary of Dylan’s sports. The days are real, but they did not happen in one stretch.
