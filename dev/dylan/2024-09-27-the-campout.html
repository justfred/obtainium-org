The Campout

September 2024 · Age 10

In the fall, when Dylan was ten, there was a campout. Dylan was in his last year at his school, and Mama thought he would love to sleep over.

Papa signed up to go with him. Mama was helping, too. She was setting up the Dippin’ Dots, the little frozen ice cream beads. But she did not want to stay the night. So it was Dylan and Papa.

There was a magic show at 6:30 that evening. Mama told Papa to head over a few minutes early so that Dylan could have a good seat. Mama also had six tickets, and she said Dylan could get something with them if he wanted.

There was a playground. Papa noticed that some of the bathrooms near it were locked and some were open. The lower ones were open. The upper ones were locked.

There was also a scary story. After the story, there was going to be a movie. Mama told Papa that he could go and get his sleeping bag after the scary story, since the movie would start after that.

Then Dylan and Papa slept out at the campout.

In the morning, Papa sent a message to his sister, Aunt Amy. It said, in big letters, “This is DYLAN and PAPA we are eating pancakes.” They were at the IHOP near the school. Aunt Amy asked, “Are you having fun camping?” She wrote back, “I love you, Dylan and Papa.”

Papa told Aunt Amy that on Sunday he wanted to go to the beach with her. She could not come. She was leaving that Sunday for a trip to Colorado and Michigan.

Mama wrote to Papa, “Thanks for camping out with Dylan.” She had a plan for the afternoon. She was going to take Dylan to check out ice hockey. It was in a town called Poway, from two to three o’clock, and Papa was invited to come along.

So it was a big weekend. First came a magic show, a scary story, a movie and a night in a sleeping bag. Then came pancakes. Then came hockey.
