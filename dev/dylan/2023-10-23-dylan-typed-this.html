Dylan Typed This

October 2023 · Age 9

Papa kept a journal on his computer. It was a running log of whatever was happening, a few lines at a time. He wrote in it from all kinds of places. Sometimes he wrote at home. Sometimes he wrote at the trampoline park while Dylan jumped. Sometimes he wrote at a coffee place or a brewery. He started a new entry each time, with the date and the time and the place.

On Monday, October 23rd, 2023, Papa wrote an entry at home at 7:04 in the evening. It began like all his entries. He wrote that he had been bitten by mosquitoes again that day, mostly on his arms and elbows. He wrote that he and Mama were both still hurting.

Then there was a line that was not like the others. Papa wrote: “Dylan typed this.”

Right after that, he wrote that Dylan was quite a computer head, between YouTube and Minecraft and his school games. He added that this was not very surprising.

The journal does not say what Dylan typed. Maybe it was just letters. Maybe it was a word. Maybe it was something longer. Papa did not copy it down. All that was left was Papa’s note, which told anyone who read it later that Dylan had been there, at the keyboard, and had made his mark on the page.

Dylan knew a lot about computers and tablets already. He played Minecraft, which he played so much that summer that Papa wrote it was “a hell of a lot.” On a plane that July, Papa wrote that Dylan entertained himself with his tablet. Dylan also liked to take videos on Papa’s phone, so many that Papa had to go through them and delete most of them. And at school, he played games on a computer.

That is how Papa knew. Dylan did not need to be taught to find his way around a keyboard.

So on a Monday evening in October, with mosquito bites on his arms and everyone hurting a bit, Papa let Dylan type into his journal. Dylan’s words are the one thing in that entry that Papa did not write himself. Nobody knows what they were, but they were Dylan’s.
