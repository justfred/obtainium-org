Youtopia

June 2023 · Age 9

In June 2023, Dylan was nine years old, and he went camping with Papa at a festival called Youtopia.

Youtopia is the San Diego area’s own Burning Man event. Burning Man is a giant gathering in the desert where people camp together, make art, and dance. Youtopia is a smaller version closer to home. It was as close as Dylan had come to Burning Man.

Papa got ready for the trip on Thursday, June 8. He wanted to get there not too late. He planned to make carne asada to bring.

Then the two of them went to Youtopia. Papa wrote that the past few days were Youtopia.

There was a place there called Danger Camp. At Danger Camp, there was lots of sword fighting. Dylan fought with the foam swords. Papa wrote in his journal that there was lots of sword fighting, and lots of walking, too.

There was a pool. Dylan got to swim in it, and Papa said that was a great break.

At night, a band called That Beatles Cover Band played. Dylan danced to the music. He danced in front of the video screen. He bounced on a couch. And he pulled people onto the dance floor to dance with him.

Papa was mostly there to keep an eye on Dylan. He also brought along part of his bottle collection and gave away about 24 of the bottles to people at the festival. That was about half of what he had left.

Papa wrote down one more thing: “We had a good time, he more so than me I suppose.” So Dylan had a great time.

And Dylan took pictures too. Papa’s photos and Dylan’s photos from Youtopia turned out well, with almost no effort. You can see many of them here: https://www.flickr.com/photos/justfred/albums/72177720309027893/
