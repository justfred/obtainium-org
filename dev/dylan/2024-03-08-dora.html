Dora

March 2024 · Dora, age 15½

Dora was a stray. That means she didn’t have a family, and nobody knew exactly when she was born. The best guess is the fall of 2008.

She started out in El Centro. El Centro is a flat, dry desert city, a long way from the ocean. It is also where Grandma Elise and Grandpa Phil lived. Somehow Dora ended up at a shelter in Encinitas, a beach town with more trees and more fog than the desert.

Mama was working at that shelter. One day she came home and told Papa about a dog. That was February 18, 2010. After that, Dora’s home was with Mama and Papa.

Dylan wasn’t born yet. Dora had almost four years to herself first. She went to obedience training, like a good dog. Papa took her to Oceanside Harbor, and they walked out along the breakwater, past the boats. They walked on Carlsbad Beach. They went to Fiesta Island in Mission Bay, a big open place with paths and grass and other dogs. Dora loved to run there. She loved chasing other dogs, which is one of the things dogs like best.

Papa wrote once that he was at Fiesta Island, walking Dora, and he thought his life was perfect. He did not mean that everything was sorted out. He meant he was exactly where he wanted to be, with a good dog, on a nice day.

Then Dylan came along, in January 2014. Suddenly there was a baby in the house. Dora got used to it. The baby turned into a boy, and Dylan grew up with a dog. That is a lucky thing. It is hard to notice that kind of luck while you are in the middle of it. In 2017, when Dylan was about three, there is a picture of him with Mama, Papa, and Dora in the wildflowers at Borrego.

In December 2023, the vet found that Dora had lymphoma. That is a kind of cancer. It meant she didn’t have much time left.

Papa’s birthday is December 13th. That year he turned fifty-eight, and he took part of the day off work. He wrote that he wanted to take Dora for a walk. He wanted to give her some time outside, in the air, while she still could. He didn’t make a big deal of it. He just took the dog for a walk. That is one quiet way of showing love.

Dora died on March 8, 2024, at home, with Mama and Papa beside her. She was about fifteen and a half, which is a very long life for a dog.

She had a good life, and a family that loved her.
