The Blue Room

Politics, explained by people who can't agree on anything. Including the facts.

★ ★ ★ ★ ★

In a country where everybody has an opinion...

...and almost nobody has the footnotes...

...one man has a bell.

He's Wonka. He hosts. He is wrong once an episode, on purpose.

Four voices. One issue. A guest chair with an actual clue. Eight minutes, give or take a conspiracy theory.

And at the end, the ledger: what's documented, what's disputed, and what's just somebody's talking point.

It's not a debate. It's a fact-check with a studio audience.

Season one · taped Sunday, November 1, 2026
  • Episode 1: Trans People in Bathrooms. An easy one. (It isn't.)
  • Episode 2: Alaska's Two Sullivans. Two men. One name. One federal lawsuit.
  • Episode 3: Noncitizen Voting and California's 20,000 Ballots. A list of twenty thousand names, and one question: who checked? Guest chair: a former county registrar of voters.
  • Episode 4: The Jack Smith Hearing. Phone records, a bell, and the wrong basketball team. Guest chair: a former Senate committee counsel.
  • Episode 5: The Epstein Files. Three million pages out, three million to go, and a missing log. Guest chair: a former federal records officer.
  • Episode 6: California's Props 40, 41 and 42. A tax, two blockers, and a rule that lets one ballot measure kill another. Guest chair: a former state budget analyst.
  • Episode 7: The Day After. Election Day, election night, the day after, the next two years. Our panel predicts. We'll check. Guest chair: a former state elections director.

Every episode ends with a ledger sorting what the sources support from what they don't, and a list of notes and links so you can check us. The facts come from news reports, court and agency pages and surveys; many came from search summaries, and each episode says which. Partisan and advocacy sources are labeled. Where a figure wasn't re-confirmed, the notes say so. The guest experts are invented, and their lines rest only on the sources in the notes.
