Biography

The Blue Room

A fictional late-night political talk show, written by Claude (an AI) as political satire. The host and guests are invented comic archetypes, not real people, and nothing a character says is the site author’s opinion. Each episode takes one issue, sorts what is documented from what is disputed or unsupported, and ends with sources.

How it works

Wonka hosts, in the style of a gentle, ironic game-show impresario, and checks the mechanics, the numbers and the sources. Four regular guests argue the issue, and some episodes add a guest chair: an invented subject-matter expert who explains how the machinery works and says “that’s outside what I can speak to” when the sources run out.

The regulars:

  • Shelby Spinner, a proud MAGA mama bear: polished, protective, talks over people and repeats her best line.
  • Walt Dunning, a retired actuary and copy editor: asks “who says so?” and “Mm. Citation?” and doubts both sides.
  • Dale Truitt, formerly of overnight radio: connects the dots, then has to say where the evidence ends.
  • Margo Bell, a cable-news-fluent liberal pundit: frames the story, and sometimes over-sells it.

Every episode ends with three lists. Documented means a source supports it. Disputed means credible people disagree, or the evidence is thin. Unsupported means a claim made on stage that no source I found backs up. The audience in the studio reacts to rhetoric; a good line can get applause without making the claim true.

Episodes

All taped Sunday, November 1, 2026, two days before the election.

  1. Episode 1: Trans People in Bathrooms. State laws, the polling, the research, and a Virginia case that both sides cite.
  2. Episode 2: Alaska’s Two Sullivans. The Justice Department’s suit over two candidates with the same name, and the disputed report that Democrats helped recruit one. Guest chair: none.
  3. Episode 3: Noncitizen Voting and California’s 20,000 Ballots. How a “likely noncitizen” list is built, why it errs, and what the courts did. Guest chair: Ruth Ellery, an invented former county registrar of voters.
  4. Episode 4: The Jack Smith Hearing. Phone records of lawmakers, a mixed-up basketball team, and what a Senate hearing did and didn’t establish. Guest chair: Lorraine Voss, an invented former Senate committee counsel.
  5. Episode 5: The Epstein Files. What was released, what was redacted, what is missing, and what Democrats say they’d do. Guest chair: Inez Calloway, an invented former federal records officer.

About the sources

The facts in each episode come from news reports, court and agency pages, and survey sources, linked in the notes at the bottom of each page. Many came from search summaries, not full pages, and each episode says which. Partisan and advocacy sources are labeled. Where a figure was not re-confirmed, the notes say so. If you spot an error, the notes are the place to check it against.
