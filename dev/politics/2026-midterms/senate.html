Bottom line

  • Now: 53 R – 47 D (45 D + 2 I who caucus D)

  • Forecasts: Silver Bulletin D 76% (~52–48) · VoteHub D 60% · DDHQ D 57% · markets D ~65%
  • Generic ballot: D+8.7 (avg of 5, Oct 22)

Realistic read

Range 49–54 D, most likely 51–52. Midterm polls have been accurate lately (2018/2022 error < 1 pt), but a 2–3 pt correlated R-ward miss → ~48–49 D. Discount polls most in AK, SC, NE, ME (history of misses / thin polling). Silver race odds (~Oct 6): NC 95% D · TX 74% · OH 74% · ME 71% · AK 65% · KS 51% R.
