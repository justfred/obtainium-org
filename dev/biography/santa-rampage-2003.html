Santa Rampage 2003 (LA SantaCon)

From Fred’s LiveJournal (as patient0), posted verbatim except for one passage he chose to remove.

2003-12-15 10:28 — Once Santa starts drinking...

itemid 13531 · visibility: public

...there’s no stopping him.

Met up with Trey and his crones at the Drawing Room in Los Feliz, rather than breakfast at House of Pies we started with Long Islands. Wandered over to the HOP, which was mobbed with Santas. Someone started throwing snoballs at passing cars, probably the first time the police really noticed us. The elves showed up with strike signs, and had a heated argument with the snowball-throwing Santas.

After a visit to the Dianetics center (the receptionist took it quite well, I think she was warned ahead of time), we rode the Metro down to Olivera street for Margaritas and Mariachis. Tried to infiltrate the mexican dancers, but they were having none of that and we weren’t tanked enough to insist. Then up to Highland Park to a dive bar called Mr. T’s for Karaoke. Some elves got in a shaving cream fight with some of the Santas. Someone set off a stinkbomb on the bowling alley/dance floor. Outside, I showed off my “Santa War Dance” (“Santa Haka”) which I thought was damned funny even tho only a few people apprecated it.

Up to Pasadena, Holly Street bar, then to City hall to worship the giant tree there. Down to the Mall. That’s where things got a little uglier. One of the santas flashed his ass for a photo, got picked up by the mall cops. Kept having this conversation with strangers:

“Who are you guys? Is this a club?” -“We’re Santa!” “Why are you doing this?” -“Because it’s Christmas!” “Where’re you from?” -“The North Pole, duh!” (etc.)

The Islands we were supposed to go was too small and we were too rowdy, so we ended up at the Yard House - brewpub. More beers, finally some food. One of the Ms. Santas got arrested for assautling a waiter - grabbing his ass or something. Apparently she deserved it. “Couldn’t have happened to a nicer person”. But then we were cut off. So off to yet another bar. (You sensing the theme here?) Along the way there was lots of bawdy caroling. Went to a massive Christmas store and hassled the people there. Finally ended up in a strip club. Only about 50 santas by that point.

All in all it was fun. Had dinner and good conversation before the strip club with Milly and Laura. Strangely, when I showed up at Cheetah’s, none of the santas looked all that familiar, the ladies on stage were quite lackluster, and I wasn’t up for a lap dance, so I gave up pretty quickly.
