Cedar Fire 2003

From Fred’s LiveJournal (as patient0), posted verbatim: his words, unedited.

2003-10-26 19:47 — Viva la Fuego

itemid 6195 · visibility: public · mood: shocked

It’s been a hell of a long day. I spent yesterday at Indy’s moving stuff around and getting things organized for the decompression party, which was planned for three weeks - or is it two weeks - from now. Meloney was there, and Jones, and Figment, and Neil and Charles showed up. Was a good productive day, and I even felt like things are getting back to normal with Meloney. Got home and got good and messed up and wrote a strange email to her, which wasn’t too offensive, I suppose, but basically said I hope we’re back to being friends.

This morning, woke up to find out that Indy’s house had burned down. A huge wildfire took most of Valley Center. And another one blazed from Ramona down to Lakeside, and across Miramar almost to the ocean; this one probably took Magnus’ house but he doesn’t know because they wouldn’t let him go back up the road to check. He’s answering through Becke’s phone because she was staying over at his place at the time. Yuh huh. Spent most of the day with Indy and Neil and Charles and Indy’s new friend whose name I can never remember but who doesn’t remotely hold a candle to Shebang. Indy’s staying over here tonight. I can still see the flames on the far ridge on the other side of Escondido, as well as over the hill to the east - a bit close for comfort but not close enough to make me put my valuables in the car. I can’t even think of what I’d throw in the car on the way out - not a bloody lot that I’d really miss, tho it would be a shame for all the stuff to go up ininsured. Journals. The laptop. Not like I want to lose everything else but it wouldn’t end my world. But it does make me consider it. A lot of petty crap goes on in the world, but then serious shit like this comes along.

Ate a couple of slices of pizza today, and I’m still down to 241 lbs. Tried to go to sleep a little while back but couldn’t. Maybe I’ll get back in the hot tub before Indy shows up.
