Korean Dinner with Kip

April 2001. Carlsbad area.

Went out to Korean with Kip on Saturday. That stuff is serious and he always orders too much — it’s like he doesn’t think about it. Then I end up paying more because I got beer and he didn’t; and then he never puts out.

Not that he should. Or that there’s anything wrong with that.
