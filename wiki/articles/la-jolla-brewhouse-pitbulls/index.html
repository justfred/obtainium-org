La Jolla Brewhouse Pit Bull Rescue

February 20, 2011. La Jolla Brewhouse.

I guess they’re having a pit bull rescue event here; I just came in because I heard it was dog-friendly. Took Dora to Ocean Beach, then to a dog wash — she’s being very well-behaved.

Yesterday was the San Diego Burning Man Film Festival, which was fun; seemed to be a successful event. About halfway through I got a headache, and Becke had already gone home, so I didn’t stay long — but it was a good time and I saw and hugged a bunch of burners. Got home and slept about 13 hours, from 8 till 9 or so, and my cold now seems to be mostly gone.

I’m wondering if some of the throat soreness can be blamed on the damned sunflower seeds — they can’t possibly be any good for me.
