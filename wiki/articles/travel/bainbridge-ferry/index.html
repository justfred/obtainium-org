Bainbridge–Seattle Ferry

August 19, 2004. Bainbridge–Seattle Ferry.

Not a lot of time to write, the ferries here are quite efficient and we’re about halfway to Seattle, but wanted to note that it’s absolutely gorgeous and the view of the city is wondrous. Staying with the Hubbards tonight, and spending the weekend in Seattle as a tourist — sometime you’re a traveller, sometimes just a tourist.
