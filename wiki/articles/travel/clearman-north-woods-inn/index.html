Throw the Peanut Shells on the Floor

On my way up to see Becke. Stopped at Clearman’s North Woods Inn in La Mirada. They seem to be particularly concerned that you throw the liberally provided peanut shells on the floor. Signs everywhere. Don’t people know that by now?

This place seems somewhere slightly more civilized than Pinnacle Peaks, not so much Denny’s as Tyler’s Taste of Texas. But I’ve been to a lot nicer steak joints, one I’m thinking of in Dallas that wasn’t bad, I might even go again if I ever got back there and remembered where it was — not far from the grassy knoll, I suppose.

All dinners include two salads. Does that mean they’re actually dinners for two? And there’s a picture on the wall above my table that features exposed nipples, which is certainly a nice touch.

…and back on down the road, not really needing to ever stop here again. But I could. If I brought along some screaming women. Never can have enough of those screaming women.

Source: Journal, May 2004
