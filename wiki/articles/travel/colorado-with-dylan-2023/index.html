Colorado with Dylan

Late June 2023. Denver, Colorado›? Springs, Pagosa Springs, Ouray›?, Telluride›?, Mesa Verde.

Dylan and I flew to Denver and drove through Colorado for about ten days in the summer of 2023. He was nine. We saw a bear, a buffalo, a wild turkey, and a marmot. Dylan played in a creek near Telluride, floating sticks and leaves, which is the correct thing to do in a mountain creek when you are nine.

The alpine slide at Colorado Springs cost a hundred and twenty dollars for four rides. Dylan thought this was a very reasonable price. I have chosen not to argue with him about it.

We went to Great Divide Brewing, which was outstanding, and to a Viking-themed bar where I had hot chicken for lunch that was just about too hot to finish. MeowWolf was enormous and strange in exactly the right proportion. The Tattered Cover bookstore had seen better days but was still the Tattered Cover.

Dylan was a good passenger. He played Minecraft on the flight and did not cause any incidents, notwithstanding some in-game polar bear violence.

We came home with a parking ticket from MeowWolf and the memory of watching Dylan float sticks in that creek. Of all of it, that’s the image that stays. Kid, water, sticks. The rest is scenery.
