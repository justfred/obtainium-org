Mom Gets an iPhone 4

November 16, 2010.

Mom just got an iPhone 4. Seems like Dad should have got that first, but it makes some sense. I’m sure the vast majority of capabilities are wasted on her — she’s talking about moving the default applications off the home screen to make it simpler, which means she does “get it” to some extent; kudos to the Apple dude, I suppose. But at the same time, it’s unlikely she’ll download an app.

I wish I could get Becke familiar with the navigation app, which isn’t the best, but at least she could use it while I drive.
