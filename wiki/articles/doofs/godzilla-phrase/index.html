The Only Way to Kill Godzilla Now

A Doofs tradition: learn to say “The only way to kill Godzilla now is with a nuclear weapon” in as many languages as possible.

This came from Todd, as most doof things did. The challenge: memorize the phrase in as many languages as you could, and deploy it at parties. The phrase itself is useless. That was the point.

From memory:

Japanese: Ima gozira o koroshu hoho wa genshi buki shika nai.

German: Das einzige Weg Godzilla jetzt zu töten, ist mit einem Atomwaffe.

Spanish: El solo posibilidad para matar Godzilla ahora, es con una bomba atómica.

Fred had a side collection of Godzilla figures. One lived on the dash of the Pinzgauer.
