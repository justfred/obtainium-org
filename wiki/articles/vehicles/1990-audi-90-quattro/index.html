1990 Audi 90 Quattro 20V

~1990–1998

I bought it on a whim after seeing it in the parking lot of a sushi restaurant with a for-sale sign. This is not a recommended vehicle purchasing strategy, but it worked out. It cornered like crazy. It was genuinely fun to drive.

I’m not really into the gofast, though, and eventually I wanted something different. I traded it — along with the motorcycle — for the first Discovery. Which was the wrong decision, but the motorcycle was also the wrong decision, so two wrongs making a right is perhaps too optimistic. Still: the Audi was a better car than either of the Discoveries.
