1990 Honda CBR650

~1990–1998

I hit 160mph on it once or twice. The problems: too hot. Too cold. Too scared of traffic. And the possibility of doing something really stupid was always very close to the surface.

I got rid of it before wisdom failed completely. Traded it along with the Audi for the first Discovery, which was itself a mistake, but at least a slower one.

Sometimes the responsible decision is recognizing when you’re having too much fun in the wrong way.
