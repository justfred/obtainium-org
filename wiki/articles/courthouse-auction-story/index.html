Please Don't Bid

November 15, 2010. Overheard.

The woman next to me was telling a story: People were at the courthouse and they were auctioning off houses, and they came to one, and put it up for bid, and a woman stood up and said, “Please don’t buy this house — it’s mine. I’m a single mother and I’m struggling to pay the bills, so please don’t bid.”

And no one bid, and she got to keep the house.
