Scolari's Food & Drug, Reno

The annual Burning Man grocery stop. Rated ★★★★☆

This is where we stop for groceries on the way to Burning Man; so I go here about once a year. It’s a great supermarket — everything I need in the way of food and personal gear, as well as water dispensers (that are often overloaded, even though they’ve been recently replaced — they should really rent a few for burner season). The produce and meat sections are overqualified for filling up a cooler. Lines are usually quick when I’ve been there, and the staff seem to be burner-friendly.
