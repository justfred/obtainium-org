JR's Barbeque

JR’s Barbeque, Los Angeles›?. Rated ★★★★★

Love this place. The BBQ beef ribs are so good I haven’t bothered to try anything else — except the 7-Up cake, which was lemon-limey and worth it.

The sauce is awesome. The hot sauce is so hot that even I thought it was hot, which is a meaningful calibration point. The guys who work there are friendly. The customers are entertaining. The decor and the neighborhood — well, the ribs and the sauce are great. That’s what matters.

Usually I get takeout so I can go home and have a beer with it.

There’s a particular kind of Los Angeles barbecue joint that runs in specific neighborhoods with consistent quality and almost no tourist awareness. JR’s is that kind of place. You know it’s right when you come in and people are getting their food to go, and everyone seems to have been here before.

Five stars. Go for the beef ribs and the hot sauce. Get the 7-Up cake if they have it.
