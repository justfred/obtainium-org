The Calexico Border Crossing

Coming back from Guadalupe Canyon via Mexicali. Zero stars for the wait, three for letting him push through. Rated ★★☆☆☆

After camping in Guadalupe Canyon, made my way back through Mexicali on Sunday mid-day. Got stopped near the border by a cop for running a stop sign that wasn’t there. The usual drill — follow me to the station — can we deal with it here — $20 mordida. Pulled into line then waited there for THREE HOURS. Right as I pulled up to the agent, I ran out of gas. They glanced at my dirty truck and our passports, then let me push the vehicle on to the American Sektor. There’s a gas station half a block away, and the usual $20 disposable plastic container. Zero stars for the pointless wait, the heat, the beggars, the greedy corrupt cops, the stench of burning garbage. Three stars for letting me push on through to the other side!
