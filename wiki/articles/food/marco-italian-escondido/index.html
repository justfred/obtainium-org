Marco Italian Restaurant, Escondido

Escondido›? Italian joint with all the right clichés. Rated ★★★★☆

This place feels like the good old Italian restaurant from the childhood in New Jersey that I never had. It has all the trademarks of fine Italian dining: dim lighting, red-and-white-checkered tablecloths, candles on the tables, burn marks on the plastic menus from the candles on the tables, a jolly Italian proprietor, paintings of Italy on the walls, sparkle stars on the ceiling, wicker-wrapped Chianti bottles all over the place.

The food is fairly simple and hearty — lots of carbohydrates and garlic. I tend to have the pizza (thin crust, plain pepperoni) and the spaghetti and ravioli combo. Seems to close for months at a time, apparently when the owners go back to the old country.
