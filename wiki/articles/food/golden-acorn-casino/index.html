The Golden Acorn Casino

A stop on the way to the desert. Fred's verdict: fill up and keep moving. Rated ★☆☆☆☆

One star because the gas prices aren’t all that bad. Zero stars added because the meal was tolerable and relatively inexpensive. I probably should have had breakfast (served anytime) but instead had the ortega burger on texas toast. Four non-stars because the place reeks of cigarette smoke, in a nasty bad horrible way. I must have gotten a years’ worth of second-hand, just walking through. Sure, it’s entertaining to see so many grotesquely overweight people in polyester throwing their money and their white trash lives away. Not quite entertaining enough to justify hacking your lungs out. My advice — fill up the tank, pay at the pump so you don’t have to go inside.
