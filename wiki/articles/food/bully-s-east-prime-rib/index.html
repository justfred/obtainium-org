Bully's East Prime Rib

San Diego›? steakhouse. Fred›?'s preferred solo dinner spot. Rated ★★★★☆

When I have an evening to myself, I like to sit at the “Low bar” — a big round table at the end of the bar where you can drink and dine. It’s a lot more social and it’s fine to sit there by yourself and chat up the other losers. As a protein fiend I like the Prime Rib (some of the best I’ve had in town, but I’m a cheapskate so I don’t go to fancy places that much), and the side asparagus (extra charge). Load on the creamy horseradish, and a beer on the side 'cause I’m not having any carbs otherwise.
