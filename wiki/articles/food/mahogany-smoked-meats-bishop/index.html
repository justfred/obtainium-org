Mahogany Smoked Meats, Bishop

Best bacon ever, Bishop CA. Rated ★★★★☆

Best bacon ever. Every time I blow through Bishop I make it a point to stop here and pick up a couple of lbs, including some to give to family. I like to cook it on the barbecue — the extra fat drips through and you end up with crispy lean bacon goodness. The jerky is good enough, though it’s pretty wet and sticky. Like the jerky? Try the bacon!

I also got the pastrami — which was good — and smoked salmon that I haven’t tried yet, and sage honey which I hope is good since the whole surrounding area smells like honey sage.
