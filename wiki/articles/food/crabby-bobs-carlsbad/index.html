Crabby Bob's Seafood, Carlsbad

March 2001. Sunday after a long Fry’s run — 40GB HD, MP3 player, Sportbrain, buttload of books from the closing Crown Books.

Crabby Bob’s. This dinner isn’t bad. Expensive, but.

They’ve got celebrity photos on the wall, but all those people can’t have been here. Maybe to the restaurant somewhere else. It’s a bit deceptive I think, or if not deceptive then at least misdirected.
