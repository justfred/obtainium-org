Ten Thousand Waves, Santa Fe

Japanese mountain spa outside Santa Fe›?. Five stars without hesitation. Rated ★★★★★

Was in Santa Fe for a wedding; we’d been up to the spa before but didn’t have time to stop, so this time we booked in advance. Beautiful grounds, feels like you’re in a Japanese mountain village. Wife and I got an outdoor tub, with a side cold dunk and a sauna. The pool was very clean, and it was private enough for her and outdoorsy enough for me. The tub was actually a lot larger than we needed — probably would have sat 20 people. Would happily go again, get a massage, maybe stay in the cabins. Good to get out of touristy (but beautiful!) Santa Fe downtown. Wish there were one of these near San Diego›?, and when I get fabulously wealthy I promise to build one!
