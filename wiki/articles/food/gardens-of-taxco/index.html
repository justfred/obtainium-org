Gardens of Taxco

Gardens of Taxco, on Harper in West Hollywood. 1980s–90s.

My favorite restaurant memory isn’t from a Michelin place. It’s from Gardens of Taxco, on Harper in West Hollywood.

The waiter would suggest dishes. When you asked what was in them, he would say, in a heavy French-Mexican accent:

“Ees like ze cheeken was borrrrrn in the sauce.”

That’s it. That’s the whole review. I’ve eaten at better restaurants since. I’ve never been told anything more useful.
