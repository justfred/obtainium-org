Fat Ivor's

Fat Ivor’s Rib Rack. Rated ★★★★☆

The place burned down a few years back and people loved it so much they rebuilt it. That tells you what you need to know.

All you can eat beef ribs, cooked on a pit barbecue. What more is there to say? The sauce is decent — the ribs are all you can eat. Not all you want to eat, mind you. All you can eat. You’re not allowed to leave if you can possibly eat another one. Beer too.

There may have been vegetables or fries or something in there. How the hell would I know. I just asked for another plate of ribs. And a beer.

Some restaurants do one thing very well. Fat Ivor’s does one thing with a kind of insistence that you stop caring about the other things that restaurants do.
