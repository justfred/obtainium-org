Penny Lane Pub & Grill

Penny Lane Pub & Grill, San Marcos. Rated ★★★★☆

Great food — I especially like the Chicken Pot Pie, or the fish and chips. Fine beers, though I usually just have a Guinness. A bit too well-lit, though that actually lends itself to getting some studying done while you sip your pint.

After the previous owner passed it on, they eased up on the Beatles paraphernalia, which is probably a good thing. The name carries the reference without having to wallow in it.

This is a good neighborhood pub in the English sense: a place where you can sit for an hour, have one good thing to eat and one good thing to drink, and feel like you’ve had a proper break from whatever you were doing. The lightness of the atmosphere is a minor complaint; the food earns the stars.
