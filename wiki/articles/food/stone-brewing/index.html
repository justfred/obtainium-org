Stone Brewing

Stone Brewing World Bistro and Gardens, Escondido. Rated ★★★★☆

I love the building and the grounds. I wish I could build my house like this — sure, brewery included.

The beers are the main attraction, and they have a great variety: their own and other high-end brews. I like the food too. Expensive, but I’ve enjoyed it as a side to whatever beer I’m having. During the summer they host a weekly outdoor movie on the grounds.

They also run “Beer University” events where you can learn more about your suds. This is the correct name for this kind of thing. If you’re going to get enthusiastic about beer, be open about the enthusiasm.

Stone is Escondido’s best argument that the San Diego inland valley is worth visiting. Before Stone existed, Escondido didn’t have a compelling reason. Now it does.

I’ve been back many times. I’ve never had a bad beer there. I can’t say that about very many places.
