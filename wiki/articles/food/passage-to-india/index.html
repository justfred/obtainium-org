Passage to India

Regular dinner spot in San Marcos. Fred and Becke’s standing order. Rated ★★★★☆

My wife and I dine here every couple of weeks. I have the Lamb Saag, she has the Tikka Masala. Both are great. She likes the “Special Naan” with coconut; I prefer mine plain. They have a decent selection of Indian beers. Seems about equal to its sister in Rancho Bernardo. Sometimes we do takeout, and when we get it home it’s a bit oily; better to have it there.
