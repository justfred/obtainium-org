Spring Street Smokehouse, Los Angeles

Spring Street Smokehouse, Los Angeles›?. Rated ★★★★☆

Great barbecue beef ribs. Amazing sauce. I tend to get it for takeout.

For my birthday one year I ordered platters and brought them to the office. This is the correct use of good takeout barbecue: feed other people with it. Ribs don’t travel particularly gracefully, but they travel well enough to get from Spring Street to an office building, and the arrival of good ribs is its own reward regardless of condition.

Don’t know about the service — they’ve always been friendly the times I’ve been there. The beef ribs are the reason to go. The sauce is the reason to order extra napkins.

Los Angeles has a real barbecue history that often gets overlooked in discussions of regional barbecue, and Spring Street is part of it. Solid four stars.
