Basil Ate a Mouse

From a letter to Mom and Dad, May 6–7, 1986. Fred is a sophomore at Pomona College.

Basil ate a mouse yesterday! I had to knock it out first, then thwack it on the head a couple of times, but he ate it.

When I go down to mail this I’m also going to drop off my passport application (at the post office), and mail a final care package — junk food — to Amy. I got a weird letter from one of her friends saying how she needed some cookies for finals week.

*Basil was my ball python at Pomona. The letter was going out with a signed credit application for an Apple computer — Dad needed to fill in the employment section. It was the spring of my sophomore year: Calculus, CS101, Physics, Accounting. Finals were spaced out nicely that year.

The passport was for the trip to Europe with Amy that summer. The mouse was not.*
