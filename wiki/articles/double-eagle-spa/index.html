Double Eagle Resort

Double Eagle Resort and Spa, June Lake area, Eastern Sierra.

We’ve been to the spa for massages twice now. The grounds are beautiful, set up against the mountains, with a stocked fishing pond. When we arrived they were running a Tai Chi class on the deck, despite the mosquitoes.

The massage was one of the best I’ve had. The staff are professional and friendly. The locker room, sauna, and hot tub were clean and comfortable. I personally liked the tacky miner decorations, which are apparently non-optional in a resort named Double Eagle in gold country.

I plan to make this a midway stop on the way back from Burning Man — get a day pass, use their showers and hot tub, decompress from the playa before the long drive home. You come down from the desert coated in alkaline dust and smelling of camp smoke and something chemical that doesn’t fully wash off for days. A proper shower and a hot tub in the mountains is the correct antidote.

The Eastern Sierra in late summer, after the burn, is one of the best drives in California. The altitude is right, the light is right, the crowds have mostly thinned.

I didn’t make it there in 2022. Next time.
