Pizza Port and the Old Brewery Bottle

April 2001. Pizza Port, which had recently been the Carlsbad Brewery.

They couldn’t refill my bottle of beer because it was from the old brewery and the liquor license won’t let them. Duh. But they’re getting me a new jug.

I’m also treating myself to some cheese fries, because darn it I’m just not fatty enough as it is.

The wind was nice when I got to the beach earlier. Actually had some minor luck with the glider — only had to run up and down the hill about half a dozen times.
