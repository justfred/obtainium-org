Kip and the Traffic School

May 2008.

Kip had a car accident in May 2008. State Farm ruled him 100% at fault.

He was teaching traffic school at the time.

There is no punchline. That’s the whole story.

Kip is one of my oldest friends — we’ve known each other since The Webb School. He is a careful, intelligent person who thinks about things. He was, at the moment State Farm needed him to demonstrate traffic expertise, demonstrating a different kind of traffic expertise.
