Wide Angle to Telephoto

September 30, 2009.

Interesting to me to notice how our brains so automatically go from wide angle to telephoto. Yesterday’s picture couldn’t fit a quarter of a rainbow in one shot; today’s you can barely see a plane that seems to my mind to fill the whole frame.
