Raley's

September 11, 2022

Raley’s, Fernley, Nevada. Rated ★★★★☆

This is my last stop fill up before Burning Man. Last time I was here it was Soleri’s, but it doesn’t seem to have changed much. First off — great quick water fill machine out front. In the past the ones inside were unpredictable and very slow. This one worked great. Inside — this store has all the groceries you’ll need. Great selection, decent prices. I bought about twice as much of everything as I ended up using. Lots of shelf stable and fresh fruits and veggies. Fair amounts of booze. Would stop here again next time!
