Webb School

Claremont, California. 1981–1984.

Webb School of California is a boarding school in the Claremont foothills — a few miles from the Claremont Colleges, which is where most of the Doofs would end up a few years later. I arrived at 14 and left at 17 with a class of 1984 diploma and a classmate named Kip who I’m still talking to forty-plus years later.

Phil's letters reference “7 years of expensive private school.” The expensive part was real. Whether it was worth it is the kind of question you stop asking once you’ve run out of counterfactuals.

The reunion in 2009 confirmed that everybody still looks like themselves, just older, and that Rick Shull now has a turquoise mine near Lee Vining.
