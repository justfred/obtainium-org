Camacho's, El Centro

El Centro institution since 1949. Fred's family has been going for generations. Rated ★★★★★

I’ve been going to Camacho’s literally all my life. Never had a bad experience. This was my parents’ favorite mexican restaurant, and my grandparents’, and my great-grandparents. Camacho’s started out in 1949 as a bar for farmhands and migrant workers, with a pool table; at some point they started serving tacos. Hasn’t changed much in the 40 years I’ve been going.

The people there are cool — if you’re from the valley you’ll almost always run into someone you know; maybe even a blue angel. It’s even inexpensive! I prefer the machaca — shredded beef (without eggs, unless you ask for them). Just meat, that’s all you need. The salsa is terrific, the side of guacamole is a must, as is cleaning the empty bowl with your finger. Special quesadillas — deep fried with great mexican cheese. Tostadas de chorizo, carne asada, plain ol’ tacos, all strongly recommended. I guess they have chicken too — but skip it and have the beef. Grab a pitcher of beer while you’re at it, you’ll need it. If you get here the wrong time of year you’ll get to smell and try to ignore the source of those yummy tacos and burritos (“olor de los vacas”); and I believe they close in August when it’s way too hot to be out there anyhow. Cash only!
