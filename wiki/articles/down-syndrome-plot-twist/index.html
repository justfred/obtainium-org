The Biggest Plot Twist

After being married for seven years, and being unsuccessful at having a kid, we had “given up.” Then my wife finally got pregnant by surprise. Several months later we found out he had Down Syndrome.

Four years later — he’s 3½, a great happy healthy kid. It’s been a strain on our marriage, and we’ve had some very difficult times, but not much different than parents of typical kids.

We discussed what to do. I know a lot of people choose to terminate, especially if (like us) they are not strongly religious. We didn’t. He goes to more therapies. It takes him a little longer to learn things like speech. It teaches us a lot of patience.

He’s happy and healthy and in most ways nearly the same as a “typical” kid.
