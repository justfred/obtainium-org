Response to the Pinzgauer Reader

February 17, 2006

Someone found the site via a search for Pinzgauer information and left comments asking pointed questions.

“Is the purpose of this site to allow fred to engage in unilateral diatribes about n’importe quoi?”

Yes.

“Who is the audience?”

Friends of mine, and anyone who happens to find it.

“And why are the posts of others ignored?”

Because there’s something wrong with the code, again. I wrote the code for this site myself, and I recently modified something that’s supposed to filter malicious data entries so hackers can’t crash my database. I saw your initial comment — it got mailed to me — but I didn’t see it on the site, so obviously something’s broken. This site is also a place for me to test web programming. It isn’t that high a priority to me.

“That hardly seems like something worth posting on a public Internet…???”

And that should matter…why? I also post most of the pictures I take, mostly so friends can look at them. It’s my server, my bandwidth. It’s not my fault that Google found it for you.

Thing is, the Internet is a virtually unlimited resource that’s essentially free. What’s “worth” posting? There’s plenty of porn, so at least there’s that. I’ve found reasonably good information about buying a car, lately. And there’s lots and lots of worthless junk like my site. It doesn’t have to be worthwhile. It’s a place for me to test PHP, and to scrawl whatever I want in a tiny little corner of the massive bathroom wall we call the internet (or occasionally “The Internets”).

In any case, thanks for visiting!
