What Burning Man Actually Costs

March 23, 2018

Everything you have.

Aside from the financial costs, which are substantial and documented endlessly elsewhere, it may cost you your job, your relationships, your sanity, or your soul. Totally worth it, though.

I’ve been going for 14-some years. Met my wife at a Burning Man event. Left jobs because they wouldn’t let me have two weeks in August for a “family vacation in the desert.” Stayed at jobs and missed it. Bought vehicles, bicycles, tents, fridges, costumes, all sorts of other crap because it would be great for Burning Man. Planned my whole year around it. Got caught up in local “regional” events. Let myself be convinced to have mind-altering experiences I wouldn’t have otherwise considered, because of it.

If all you see is a few dollar signs you’re missing a much, much bigger picture.

The only requirement is that you have a ticket. But there are many other factors you should do, including reading, understanding, and agreeing to live by the 10 principles. Practice camping. Make sure you’re bringing the right gear. And then: “Buy the ticket, take the ride.”
