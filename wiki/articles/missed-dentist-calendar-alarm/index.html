I Missed My Dentist Appointment

I missed my dentist’s appointment today, due to a combination of factors:

  • it was scheduled six weeks ago
  • they didn’t call me yesterday to confirm (even the cleaning people call to confirm)
  • my calendar doesn’t automatically show me what’s happening today, and I don’t have enough stuff on it (even tho I’m busy) that I check it all the time
  • I somehow set an alarm for 1 week AFTER the event. Why the hell would you want to set an alarm after an event?
Source: Journal, April 2004
