1963 Steyr-Puch Haflinger

January 1, 1999

~1999–2001

A companion to the Pinzgauer. If you own a Pinzgauer and think “you know what this needs,” you get a Haflinger.

It was street legal but terrifying at 40mph. I barely drove it. The top speed was the top speed, and the vehicle made sure you knew it.

I sold it with the Pinzgauer for house down payment money. I really should have kept it. It’s the one I think about.
