Portland Trip, McMenamins

May 2000. Fred is planning a trip to Portland with Felicia, hoping to stay at the famous McMenamins Edgefield.

000517.2100 Home

That was a grueling morning at CYTP — and this afternoon I managed to get nothing at all done.

I’ve got my tickets for Portland figured out. Should be fine — I would like to try to get a room at McMenamins for Saturday and Sunday nights, if I can. Maybe see a movie, have some brews.

Fair number of toys I’m taking to Hawaii — GPS, camera, PC, besides this thing — but I don’t think I’ll bother with the CD player, not much point.

So Felicia and I are going to Portland, unless for some reason the two tickets have vanished in the past hour; then tomorrow I’ll try to get a res at Edgefield and I think we can have a fine time. And I can relax and have a minivacation.

Thinking of taking all my bumper stickers and putting them in the back of the Pinzgauer — on the inside of the tailgate.
