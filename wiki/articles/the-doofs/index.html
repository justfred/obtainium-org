The Doofs

January 1, 1988

Claremont, 1988 — present.

The name doesn’t mean anything. It’s our version of “the usual gang of idiots” — a group of people who met at the Claremont Colleges around 1988 and have been failing to fully lose touch ever since. Most of the men went to CMC, most of the women to Scripps, Dave Greensfelder to Pitzer, and Bob Boardman spun in via Julie Lyss — Jewish summer camp connection, no Claremont degree required.

The diaspora — California, Seattle, Wisconsin — hasn’t killed it. The Zoom helps. Dooffest helps more.
