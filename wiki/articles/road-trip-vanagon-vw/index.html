The Longest Solo Drive

San Diego to LA to Santa Barbara to Monterey to San Francisco to Mendocino to Eugene to Portland to Olympic Peninsula to Seattle to Spokane to Idaho to Montana to Nevada and back to San Diego. Nearly all secondary roads, in an old VW Vanagon, stopping at streams and craft breweries and hot springs and little restaurants and state park campsites and Burning Man. I think I was on the road a month.
