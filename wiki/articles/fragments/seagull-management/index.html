Seagull Management

August 29, 2010. Tidewater Tavern, Solana Beach.

Steve is probably for it in theory but rarely actually works with us, though at least he sits in now and then. Nick is quite the opposite — not that he’s fighting us at every turn, but more that he’s never there to participate, and when he does it’s in seagull fashion.

Just because it stinks, doesn’t mean you have to roll around in it.
