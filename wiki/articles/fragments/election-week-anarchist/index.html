The Election That Would Not End

This is the week of the presidential election that would not end. And I’ve been spending it in New Orleans and (mostly) Pittsburgh. Tired of checking CNN to see more mixed news — Bush is still ahead but Gore will be disputing the results; in any case the election will be nondecisive for a long time, and even after it’s settled will be no great power for the winner. Truly the results an anarchist would love.

Source: Journal, November 2000
