Cargo Cult Agile

January 20, 2011. The Counter, Carlsbad.

The phrase that’s been going through my head all morning has been “cargo cult.”

You can pick pretty much any article about “cargo cult agile” off Google and it shows a picture of us.

I decided to come here for lunch not necessarily because I wanted a fancy burger, but because it’s a nicer place to sit and think than the Which Wich, which would have been my first choice.

That was a fine lamburger. I should have probably gone swimming instead.
