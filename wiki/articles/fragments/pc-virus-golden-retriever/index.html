The PC-Virus-Dog Analogy

Clearing a PC of viruses and spyware is like bathing a long-haired Golden Retriever that’s been rolling in burrs. And tar. And cat poop.

Running a PC firewall is like trying to keep the same dog from doing it again.

Kinda makes you want to own a lizard. (Or at least, a penguin!)
