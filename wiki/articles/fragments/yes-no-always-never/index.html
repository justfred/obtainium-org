YES NO ALWAYS NEVER

Today for lunch I went to the ATM and the gas station. The ATM always asks me “Do you want a printed receipt?” And I always say yes. Then at the gas station I pay with a credit card, and it wants to know if I want a receipt.

Why can’t these machines have options of ALWAYS and NEVER in addition to YES and NO? I would think most people who want a receipt ALWAYS want a receipt, and those who don’t, NEVER do. Certainly this would be easy for the bank to add (as well as a selection of PREFS from the main menu to change it back).

And while I’m thinking about it — back when we had “regular” (leaded) and “unleaded” gas, whose idea was it to call standard unleaded “regular”? Was it that hard to come up with a different word, or just call it “unleaded”? In any case: Viva Nova Sin!
