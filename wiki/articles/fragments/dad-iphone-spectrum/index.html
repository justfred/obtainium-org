Dad's iPhone and Spectrum

Dad needed a new iPhone. Went to Spectrum. Didn’t remember his Apple ID or Yahoo password, so they set him up with new ones. He lost every email he’d ever sent or received, every backup, every contact — and had phone trouble every day until he died. I tried repeatedly to get the old accounts back. Couldn’t.
