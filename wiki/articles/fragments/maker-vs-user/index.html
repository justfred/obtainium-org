Maker vs. User

August 2, 2009. At a rave.

Maker vs. user — people who spend their energy customizing but never get around to actually using the things they’ve built. There is merit in making for the value of making, but somehow I feel obligated to use — and often I don’t. Especially re: technology.

Case in point: journalling. Funny that I have to go to a rave before I get around to it.

Often the best pictures are the ones you take in your head.
