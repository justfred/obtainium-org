CPP Porter Evenings

Summer 1999. Carlsbad Pizza Port.

Man oh man that beer always tastes good. Went for a long swim this afternoon — the water is warm enough to stay out in for half an hour or so; though I realize when I get out my back is cold.

The beer is still good, fortunately, though it looks as if they’ve added some new vats but perhaps not used them yet.

I emphatically disagree with Dan that “ice cold” is how a beer should be served. Maybe if the beer tastes like piss.

Mmph, that’s a mighty good porter. $3 for a nice pinta porter is not bad. How many times will I sit here sipping my beer and clacking away at the keyboard? Seems like a repeat of a repeat of a repeat of…

Guess it’s near on time to go home. Pint is dregs, brain is mercifully empty.
