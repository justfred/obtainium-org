Todd Mansbridge

One of the founding pillars of the Doofs, the loose confederation of Claremont friends who’ve stayed in orbit since 1988.

Todd went to CMC and has been with Consilio LLC in Pasadena as Director of Pricing. Married Cynthia Unruh. Widely credited as the source of most Doofs traditions — including the Godzilla phrase — and the originator of the Guinness Theory: the first pint tastes like a Guinness, the second really tastes like a Guinness, and by the fourth it tastes like a root beer float.
