Pamarie

Trauma therapist. “A bit crazy in mostly good ways.” Long-time friend.

She came to my office to have me look at her computer — something I hate to do. She was interesting enough that it started a friendship. That was over twenty years ago.

She’s a trauma therapist in Ashland, Oregon. A Burner, more occasionally than me, though she’s been going nearly as long.

In 2025 she flew down to San Diego›? and drove to Burning Man with me. We took a playa walk together during the event.

A friendship that has lasted twenty-plus years across distance, life changes, and the general entropy of adulthood is its own kind of accomplishment.
