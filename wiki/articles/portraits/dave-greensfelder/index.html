Dave Greensfelder

Pitzer College (Claremont Colleges) class of 1988 — technically not CMC but close enough for Doofs purposes.

Dave founded and runs Greensfelder Real Estate Strategy in Albany, CA, where he is Managing Principal. He went from the Claremont bubble to building a boutique real estate strategy firm, which tracks.
