Nicholas Corwin

Webb School classmate and lifelong friend. The friend Fred›? has known the longest.

Born Nicholas Corwin; went by Kip through his Webb years and for decades after — people teased him: “Kip, kip, kip!” He eventually changed his name to Nicholas. Webb friends, the Doofs, and Fred’s family still call him Kip. I suggested “Starship Captain James Tiberius Corwin.” He chose Nicholas for his own reasons.

Grew up in New York City on Riverside Drive. His father Marvin Corwin was a Madison Avenue adman — one of the original Mad Men era figures, connected to the “I’d like to buy the world a Coke” campaign. His mother is Ellen Zimmerman›?, who is 92 and still worth talking to.

He picked a German-language book off my shelf at some point. That was enough. He taught himself German, got a law degree, got licensed in three states, couldn’t stand arguing over money, and now lives in Germany with his wife Annette as a German-to-English translator. This is a coherent life path if you follow it step by step.

We’ve agreed to drop the preface “I’ve probably told you this story before,” since we invariably have and if we left out those stories we’d have nothing to talk about whatsoever.

~767 emails plus letters from 1985 onward. He’s pedantic, academic, verbose, near-eidetically precise about dates, very self-centered, intolerant of others, and loyal in his way. He’s an expensive date — always orders too much, doesn’t split the bill. Said with affection, but it comes up.

He came to Burning Man with me in 1997 — my first, his only — and hated it. He took long hikes off-playa and rode his bike all the way to Gerlach.

I don’t quite trust him to drive the car.
