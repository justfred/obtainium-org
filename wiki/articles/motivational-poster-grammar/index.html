The Motivational Poster

I saw a nice, framed and matted motivational poster on the doctor’s wall, that said: “Work as you don’t need the money / Love as if you’ve never been hurt / Dance as if no one’s watching…” and so on.

But it was missing the “if” in the first line.

I had to point it out; now whenever they happen to notice that poster hopefully it’ll annoy them like it annoyed me.
