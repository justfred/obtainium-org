The Sparkle Ponies

A Quora answer, 2019. Someone asked about the sexiest women at Burning Man.

Someone asked me on Quora: “What are the most stunning photos of the sexiest women from the Burning Man festival?”

I wrote back:

"The hottest women at Burning Man are the ones that do all the work behind the scenes, and aren’t out posing for photos. DPW, Rangers, Gayte, Perimeter, medical, artists, chefs, coffee and ice workers, the woman with a sledge in her hand pounding rebar, the one out on open playa picking up MOOP…

These are the goddamn fucking sexiest women at Burning Man. The ones in the photos? We have a term for them: ‘Sparkle Ponies.’"

This is a thing I believe genuinely. The most interesting people at any event are the ones you have to find, not the ones performing for the camera. The Sparkle Pony phenomenon isn’t unique to Burning Man — it’s a festival-scale version of a more general principle about who actually makes things work versus who looks good in the pictures.

Also, I will confess, pounding rebar on the playa at 100 degrees is genuinely impressive. Try it sometime before you form opinions.
