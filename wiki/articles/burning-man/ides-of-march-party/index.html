The Ides of March Party

2003. Fred›? and friends go to Buca de Beppo in togas. Their waiter is Reginus Maximus.

The Ides of March party! We went to Buca de Beppo in togas. When a caesar salad went by we’d say “Hail Caesar!” Our waiter was Reginus Maximus. Later we went to the Forum and recited “Friends, Romans, countrymen…”
