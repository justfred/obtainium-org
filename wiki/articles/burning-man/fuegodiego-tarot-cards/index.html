The Fuegodiego Tarot Cards

2003. San Diego Burning Man community / Black Rock City.

Back when the Iraq Most Wanted deck came out, I thought it would be an interesting idea for personal cards for people in our group — but it just isn’t imaginative enough, and doesn’t fit the theme. Felicia did tarot and helped me understand that I wasn’t going to be causing havoc on a global scale by modifying them (in fact, later I learned that was the tradition of how tarot decks used to be created, with the royalty and famous people portrayed as characters).

I made up a couple of samples showing people from our group. All these people have “playa names,” you see. Don’t worry, I’m still justfred.

I showed off the tarot samples with the hope that people would learn a bit of Photoshop, enough to do their own cards, and we would have some to hand out, and it would be cool. Turned out about half a dozen people did their own cards, and I learned a lot of Photoshop (Elements) and did almost all the cards; and everyone who showed up regularly chose a card, so with a few wildcards and camp cards we had a full deck of 78, of which I printed about 100, along with personal cards to give away.

Many of them were just cropped heads (some in more ways than others) but some we did poses and costumes. They turned out rather amazing, I’m not ashamed to say. We got done the week before the burn; I picked up the printed cards as I was heading out of town.

We didn’t limit it to the Albano-Waite deck, so some people wanted cards from the Crowley Thoth deck, but I let someone else do the editing on those — I may be foolhardy but I’m not insane, so far as I know.
