Dalton's Roadhouse, San Marcos

Early 2000, Vista era. Fred is sorting out his living situation, working at Telenomics.

000120.1934 Dalton’s Roadhouse San Marcos

So I’ve been busy making rash decisions, and I’m celebrating by having a piggy dinner. It feels good, though, to have come up with some answers. I’m not moving out of the house in Vista until I have to. Instead of spending $1200/month on a rental, I’m going to shell out a little on a storage unit.

I’m buying a Sony 505 PC — specifically, a PCG-N505VE; at 2.7 lbs with a normal screen, trackpad instead of a finger tip mouse, and a bit faster and larger HD, and it’s several hundred dollars less. Too bad none of the PCs have network support built in. It does have a built-in modem, and it’s really really thin!

Kathie emailed to say that Gramp wants to die, would really like to stop the treatments and just fade away — and we’re not sure whether Gram would really notice. Sad — makes me feel as if we’ve abandoned them.

Need to have my tuxedo cleaned again. Ho hum.
