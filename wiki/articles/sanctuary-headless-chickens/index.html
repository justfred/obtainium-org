Whole Farm of Headless Chickens

September 8, 2009. Post-Burning Man.

I guess working with Sanctuary prepared me to come back and work with people in crisis. It’s like a whole farm of headless chickens. No major problems acknowledged but lots of meaningless little problems to panic over.

“Sanctuary” was Fred’s camp at Burning Man 2009, which ran as a support space for people in distress.
