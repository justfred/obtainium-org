Ault Nathanielsz

January 1, 1966

Pomona College. Doofs core. Amateur marine biologist. Europe with Brender and two girls.

Fred met him at Pomona, in Smiley Hall. Swimmer and water polo player; friends with Thomas. Became one of the Doofs through Fred. “Grew old and wise a lot earlier than the rest of us” — meant as a compliment. Amateur marine biologist; Fred visited tide pools with him and Diane Cordingley. Married Brenda Killen (“Brender”) — career NGO worker, very smart; two daughters. Lived in Paris as of 2009; later Switzerland. Letters from 1988 onward — one of the longer correspondence records in the Doofs. Lots of photos with Ken. One of Fred’s closest epistolary friends; the recipient of some of Fred’s most reflective letters.
