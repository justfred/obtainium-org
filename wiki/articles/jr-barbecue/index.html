JR's Barbeque

JR’s Barbeque, Los Angeles. Rated ★★★★★

Love this place. The BBQ beef ribs are so good I haven’t bothered to try anything else — except the 7-Up cake, which was lemon-limey and worth it.

The sauce is awesome. The hot sauce is so hot that even I thought it was hot. The guys who work there are friendly. The customers are entertaining. The decor and the neighborhood — well, the ribs and the sauce are great. That’s what matters.

Usually I get takeout so I can go home and have a beer with it.

Five stars. Go for the beef ribs and the hot sauce. Get the 7-Up cake if they have it.
