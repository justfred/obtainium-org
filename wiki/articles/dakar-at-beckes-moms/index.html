Dakar Rallye at Becke's Mom's

January 14, 2007. San Diego.

Whaddya know — the Dakar Rallye is on the cable here, so I can get my fix while Becke continues to sort some of her dad’s bills. Another fun-packed weekend down here.

Went to see Amy, Dave, Chloe, and Lydia yesterday. Kip last night. Mom and Dad this morning at Seaport Village.

“Do you think I’m just anyone? Do you?” — Lawrence of Arabia.

I’m getting more nervous about not having a job, but at the same time I’m looking forward to some free time. Might even take some more flight lessons, if and when I can find my logbook.
