First Facebook App

December 2007. Fred releases TriviaFix, a Facebook app, then immediately complains about Facebook apps.

Just released his first Facebook app — TriviaFix!

Three days later: Hates apps that force you to invite friends before you even know what the heck they do.

(The app was a trivia game tied to the Buzztime trivia network. Fred spent about eight years at NTN Buzztime — the company that ran Buzztime — as a developer and eventually CTO.)
