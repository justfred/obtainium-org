Spring Street Smokehouse, Los Angeles

Spring Street Smokehouse, Los Angeles. Rated ★★★★☆

Great barbecue beef ribs. Amazing sauce. I tend to get it for takeout.

For my birthday one year I ordered platters and brought them to the office. This is the correct use of good takeout barbecue: feed other people with it.

Don’t know about the service — they’ve always been friendly the times I’ve been there. The beef ribs are the reason to go.

Solid four stars.
