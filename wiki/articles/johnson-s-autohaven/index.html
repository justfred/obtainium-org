Johnson's Autohaven

The Burning Man community’s auto shop of choice. Rated ★★★★☆

This is the auto shop of choice among San Diego “Burners.” Mark works on cars of ALL sorts — he even figured out the dual carbs on my air-cooled Pinzgauer. He’s also helped build art cars, hosted gatherings, and is an all-around great guy. Prices are very reasonable. Great place to take your hoopty.
