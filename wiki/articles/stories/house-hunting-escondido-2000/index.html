House Hunting, Escondido 2000

February 2000. Fred›? is hunting for his first solo rental in the North County SD area, trying to escape living with Amy›? and Dave.

000207.1809 Dalton’s Roadhouse

I guess maybe I’ll just have a burger. I hope I’m almost done with my house hunt. The more I do the more I think I should really be buying — if I could afford it which hopefully I will be able to in a year or so. Looked at a place just now, up in Northern North Escondido, pretty damned doggy. I think the one I like the best so far is the one up on the hill in Escondido. Decent price, some parking, great view.

The Pinzgauer›? will have to live outside, but I think it can stand it — should be a bit drier mornings being closer to the ocean. There’s a big garage and a small kitchen.

000209.1842 Marco’s Italian

I’ve got a place to live! The house on the hill in Escondido with the doggy carpet and the weird shower and the decent view. Now I’ll need to tell Amy I’m moving; discuss bookshelves; move cable and phone; order movers.

Also fantasizing about having a little jacuzzi — guess I could go look at one after I’ve found washer, drier, fridge. Too bad the Pinz will have to live outside.

Living Room: GG sofa, large bookshelves, recliner, TVs. Den. Guest Room. Bedroom: GG chest of drawers. Kitchen: fridge needed. Garage: camping stuff, car stuff, tools. Build a workbench?

I never did get the Quadra to work, maybe it’s a bad net card. Not going to worry too much about it.
