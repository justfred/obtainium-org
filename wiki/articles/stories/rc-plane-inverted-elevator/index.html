Maiden Flight with the Elevator Upside Down

Had the maiden flight of my new plane this afternoon — one fucked-up flight because the elevator was upside down, including a fine inadvertent loop, then two decent flights. The first ended partway down the hill; the other ended smashing against the fence but not quite going onto the road, which would have really really sucked. As it was there seems to be just a bit of repairable damage to the tail boom.

Didn’t get to fly the Zagy because there wasn’t enough wind for it. But I think it should do better now that there’s less weight.

I wrote that off as success enough, stopped at Carlsbad Pizza Port afterward for pizza and a beer, and called it a day.

May 29, 2001. The “Zagy” was a Zagi flying wing — designed for slope soaring, not for calm days on the Carlsbad bluffs.
