Becke Passes Her Vet Tech Exam

December 5, 2011. Board and Brew, Carlsbad.

Frustrating morning — working on trying to decipher how Strimpel’s mobile jQuery works. I never did manage to understand it, but I did get it to work, so I’m running ahead with that.

Becke passed her test! Not sure who I can or should tell, but she’s very happy to be over it. I should do something really special for her — not sure what that would be, but maybe I’ll figure it out this afternoon.

She’d been working through vet tech school while I was at Buzztime, doing externships, anatomy, the works. The exam mattered.

July 21, 2011 — she finished vet tech school. So proud of her for working so hard. Now comes the really challenging part: finding a job she likes and paying off the loan.

December 22, 2011 — Becke got a job! She hasn’t accepted yet, because she’s got another interview she’d like to do before she commits, but it sounds like she’s going to take the one at the Humane Society. $17 an hour or so isn’t a lot, but at least it’s something — help to pay off her student loans for a while.
