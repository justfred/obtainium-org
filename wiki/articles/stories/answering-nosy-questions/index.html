On Answering Unwanted Personal Questions

A tactical observation.

Someone once asked me how I handle it when people ask questions that are none of their business.

I go ahead and answer directly, with added details, far more personal than they were expecting. I invent repulsive stories if necessary. I make them regret having asked.

This technique works on most people. The uncomfortable inquirer retreats, you’ve technically answered the question, and the exchange ends faster than it would have with a polite deflection that invites follow-up.

The weakness of the technique is that occasionally you encounter someone who finds the repulsive details genuinely interesting. These people are rare but they exist. When you meet one, you’ve made a friend, which was probably not what you were trying to do, but there it is.

The rule I’ve developed: polite questions get polite answers, and questions that are none of your business get the truth, elaborated.
