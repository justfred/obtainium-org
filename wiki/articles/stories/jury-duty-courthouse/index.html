Jury Duty at the Vista Courthouse

September 13, 2010. Vista Courthouse.

Been stuck here for three and a half hours, waiting to see if I’ll be called for a trial. Not really sure if I want to be, or not — I’m pretty sure I would be dismissed if I were called up, but at least I’d feel like coming down here wasn’t a total waste of time.

I’ve watched two half movies, thanks to Frank — the silly hot tub one and the Watchmen, which I’m not really getting into. Made a halfhearted attempt to read the LA Times, and did the sudoku. Thing is I’d really rather be at work, since I was getting into the VB removal project. Shrug — it’s not like I ever take a sick day.

2:06 PM, José’s Tacos:

Got out early, didn’t even get called into a courtroom. Guess that’s because I didn’t have time to serve in a long-ass trial without being paid?

Been able to get some work done. Doesn’t seem like a lot of people are around — there are a few bugs piling up on 2.0.0, but I’m screwing around with vbfree because it’s more interesting.
