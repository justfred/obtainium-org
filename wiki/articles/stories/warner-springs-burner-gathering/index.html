Warner Springs Winter Burner Gathering

A winter burn gathering at Warner Springs. Hamburgers, pancakes, and unexpected guests.

Warner Springs winter burner gathering. We had hamburgers and pancakes so we made “cows in comforters.” Neil and Charles were there, Marie, Trey Goesh — and then Milly and Laura showed up! I had no idea they were coming or knew anyone there — but they knew Trey through Gigsville.
