Still Not Sure What My Schtick Will Be

June 30, 1999. Carlsbad Pizza Port. First Burning Man planning notes.

Still thinking about Burning Man. Still not sure what my schtick will be, if any.

I think one of the accessories I’d like to take is a chalk line — then make Nazca lines, or crop circles on the playa. Better if there are different colors of chalk. Take a plumb line so I can do ordered circles, and find some way to do a long straight line. Preferably in the middle of the night or early morning.

Speaking of which, I found a little wagon that I’d like to hook a motor up to and turn into a go-kart.

Still not sure if I’ll take the Pinzgauer, though of course I really have to. Maybe get a new set of spark plug wires and have the lubes drained and refilled first. Go slow, maybe camp somewhere on the way — it would be more pleasant to take two days.

1999 was Fred’s first year at Burning Man. He went with the Pinzgauer, which did make it. The Nazca lines did not happen. The go-kart wagon did not happen. The Pinzgauer happened every year for the next decade.
