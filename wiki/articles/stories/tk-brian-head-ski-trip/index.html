TK Under the Bridge Sign

Ski trip to Brian Head with Mom and Dad. TK›? — Thomas Kendall — standing on the roof of the Amigo under a “14’0"” bridge clearance sign, double peace signs.

The clearance sign was not incidental. TK saw a sign giving a height restriction and immediately understood the photographic opportunity: man standing on roof of vehicle, sign confirms he has the correct clearance. The logic is impeccable.

TK died in 2016. He was the first friend I made at Webb, and he would have understood the joke on the roof completely, which is why he made it.
