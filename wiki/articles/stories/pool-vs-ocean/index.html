Pool vs. Ocean

A friend at work asked what the difference was between swimming in a pool and swimming in the ocean.

You come out of the pool smelling like chlorine; you come out of the ocean smelling like salt. At the pool you might get athlete’s foot but you don’t get sand on your feet. At the pool you come out thinking, “Wow, what a great workout”; at the ocean you come out thinking “Wow, I didn’t die today.”
