Ault Signs the Wall

Early 1990s. Fred talks Ault into signing a room he’d just painted.

Ault, back to the camera, curled in the corner of a room he’d just painted. He signed it — I’d talked him into it. It was his girlfriend’s house. She had a kid not his; it didn’t last. He loved her but life was difficult.
