San Diego Burning Man Film Festival

February 20, 2011, San Diego›?. Not going that year, but still in it.

Went to the San Diego Burning Man Film Festival on February 20. Saw many friends. Got a headache. Becke went home early.

The community event exists for years when people aren’t going, or haven’t gone recently, or need to be reminded that the thing they’re part of persists between Augusts. You see the faces and remember the context. Someone will show up in the same costume they wore on the playa, slightly adjusted for a San Diego February evening. There’s footage from previous years on a screen somewhere.

It functioned as a community maintenance event: the network stays warm. You confirm you still know these people. The plans that are forming for 2012 — and there were plans for 2012; CORE was already thinking about the Caroushell — get discussed in the parking lot.

The headache was probably from the noise. That part’s in the journal with the same matter-of-fact tone as everything else.

Not going to the event is a thing you can do for a season. The community is something else: it persists, and the film festival exists to tend it during the years between burns.
