Polymorphism

February 25, 2004. Mesa College, Systems Analysis class.

The people are discussing the essence of the word “polymorphism.” Really, it’s just a buzzword — it doesn’t perfectly define what they mean — but what’s more important is to define what they really mean. The book, for example, seems to me more and more like a load of crap.

Also funny that the group that formed the other night in the Systems class is me and three women. One of whom is friends with Marie-Pierre. Wonder if she’ll tell her about the wedding dress. Every now and then I fantasize about coming to class in drag.
