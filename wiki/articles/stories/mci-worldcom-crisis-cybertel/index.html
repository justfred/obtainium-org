The MCI/WorldCom Crisis

June 2001. Cybertel is hit with an $8 million MCI bill they can’t pay. Fred›? is in the middle of it.

010601.1937 Home

So I found out today that Cybertel has a $1m/month commitment to MCI Worldcom for traffic; and so we owe them some $8m. Surprised it’s not more. We’ve apparently got to make a decision by Tuesday on how to pay them back or maybe they’re going to shut us down. Should be interesting; but my guess is that the doors won’t get shut on Tuesday.

And that my stock problems are nothing compared to the other shit Cybertel and Mangiarelli has been pulling. At least stockholders aren’t personally liable.

010604.1913 Home

This was a long day, and I suspect tomorrow will be even longer. My first clue is that I’m supposed to be there at 0500 (or earlier) to see if Global Crossing could deal with my data in their format for switchover from MCI. That we’re trying to sneak away with as many as we can get away with before MCI shuts us down. And I wonder what sort of lawsuit or liens MCI will be filing against us.

010605.2019 Home

Indeed it was a long day today; about 13 hours at work. Far as I know, we got the MCI stuff all sent to GX. What a crock of shit. So I’m tired but not tired enough to go to bed yet; I’m baking a pie (from a box, of course!) while I install the OSX development software.
