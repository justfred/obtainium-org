On Guns in the House

From a Quora answer, 2021.

In fifty years, no one has ever broken into my home — or the homes of any of my family — while we were there.

In fifty years, three of my family members have committed suicide with their guns, and one killed his family.

We are safer without guns in the house. I defend my wife and child by not having guns.

This isn’t an argument I make to change anyone’s mind. It’s a statement of the math as I’ve experienced it, in my specific family, across my specific lifetime. Your math may be different. Mine isn’t.
