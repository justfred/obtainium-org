Fred›? and Kip›? at Rick Shull’s wedding in Lee Vining. Rick’s dad Blaine was a senior executive at Hughes. Rick was a surprise baby, a problem child, a Webb escapee. Now he lives near there and has a turquoise mine.

Kip and I are both arms-crossed, both in hats, both in sunglasses. We knew exactly what we were doing.

There’s a separate photo from the same event: TK›? — Thomas Kendall — standing on the roof of the Amigo under a “14’0"” bridge clearance sign, double peace signs. The clearance sign was not incidental. TK saw a sign giving a height restriction and immediately understood the photographic opportunity: man standing on roof of vehicle, sign confirms he has the correct clearance. The logic is impeccable.

Rick’s wedding in Lee Vining, near Mono Lake and the Tioga Pass entrance to Yosemite, was the kind of gathering that ends up in multiple people’s photo archives for different reasons. For me: the arms-crossed look with Kip. For everyone: TK on the roof.

TK died in 2016. He was the first friend I made at Webb, and he would have understood the joke on the roof completely, which is why he made it.
