Thomas at Training

December 1989. Postcard from Thomas. Iwo Jima statue on the front, Arlington National Cemetery. Return address: Virginia.

"Dear Fred —

Life is Hell. Got your letter & had a grin on my face for a few min. though. — I wonder if Chenowith would sell us one of those Fast Attack vehicles. I don’t know if I’m going to make it through here — so far so good on all ‘graded, weighted events’ BUT I’ve been having a real hard time staying out of trouble with the sergeants. This is not a good thing. Will try to call late[r].

Bye"

He’d written so far down the card the last line got cut off at the edge.

Thomas was somewhere in the Virginia area, in some kind of structured training program with graded weighted events and sergeants. The Chenowith reference was completely Thomas: those are the Fast Attack Vehicles, the desert patrol dune buggies that special operations used. Purely on-brand for two car-obsessed guys to wonder if they could buy one.

He got the grin. I was glad.
