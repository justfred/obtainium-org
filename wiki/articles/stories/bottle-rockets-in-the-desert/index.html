Bottle Rockets in the Desert

~Early 2000s. A dry lake on the California desert border of Fort Irwin Marine base.

A friend and I went to camp on a dry lake in the California desert. We were supposed to meet another friend out there but couldn’t find him in the dark. So we set up a small camp, had dinner, had a small campfire, went to sleep next to the cars.

Very early the next morning: incoming bottle rockets.

Our friend had arrived in the middle of the night. This was his announcement.

He had found us in the dark on a vast, featureless, lightless dry lake in the middle of the Mojave. To my knowledge, he never explained how.
