Kip at the Korean Restaurant

Went out to Korean with Kip›? on Saturday. That stuff is serious and he always orders too much — it’s like he doesn’t think about it. Then I end up paying more because I got beer and he didn’t; and then he never puts out. Not that he should. Or that there’s anything wrong with that.

That’s the thing about Kip. He is what he is, completely and without apology. One thing about Kip: he’s an expensive date.

April 22, 2001. Korean BBQ is expensive when ordered by someone who doesn’t drink and therefore doesn’t clock the check. Fred noted this pattern without apparent intention of stopping.
