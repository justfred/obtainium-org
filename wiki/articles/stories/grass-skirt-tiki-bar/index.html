The Grass Skirt, Pacific Beach

November 3, 2019. Pacific Beach.

Grass Skirt, Pacific Beach. Got a Professor Feathers — served in a bird glass — and a Painkiller that I was still nursing. Double bao. The crowd was starting to pick up when I got there. Couldn’t find the door when I came in.

“Next time I come here, I think I could easily wear a dress and not be worried.”

The Grass Skirt is a tiki bar in Pacific Beach — dark, ornate, committed to the aesthetic in the way that the good ones are. Pacific Beach is comfortable territory: young, coastal, relaxed about most things. The clientele at a tiki bar in that neighborhood has self-selected for the kind of person who walked past the regular bar to get to the one with better rum.

I walked around afterward. Checked out Belmont Park, which is kind of a crap hole but has its charms. Met a man named Victor, or something beginning with V, who was collecting flotsam from the beach and attaching it to his house. We talked for a while.

The Professor Feathers was tall and good. The Painkiller was still going by the time I left. A bar that serves rum in a bird glass has its priorities in order.
