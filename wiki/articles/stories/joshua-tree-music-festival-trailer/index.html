Joshua Tree Music Festival with the Trailer

May 2012.

Had a long weekend — went camping at Joshua Tree for the music festival. Wasn’t all that impressed with the music or the festival actually, but the camper worked fine and it was good to get out. I’m out of practice camping, I guess. Camped and partied with Chris and Sally — they slept in the front bed of the trailer. Wasn’t bad, though when someone gets up the whole thing moves a little.

I managed to tear a hole in the canvas sliding the bed in, and it was a heavy tow, but otherwise it worked out fine.

Next weekend I’m camping by myself for three nights — with ten thousand of my closest friends, but not Becke. Will try to organize better. I think I’ll take a tiny tent, a popup, and try to sleep on the roof. Not sure what to bring for food — simple stuff, I’ll probably be fed by the Rangers as well. Hope it’s cooler out there than it was in the desert. I assume it should be.

The trailer made it. That was the main question.
