Metro Red Line, Los Angeles

January 23, 2007. Metro Red Line, Los Angeles›?.

Another worthless BBCOM day — but it’s been raining jobs. Got two interviews lined up tomorrow — not necessarily for jobs that I’m going to want, but it’s good practice to start talking to people.

“Between us — it’s a startup company that hasn’t grown legs.”

I had forgotten how useful it is to have one single computer that you just drag around with you, using it whenever you have the need. I still think it would be better if it were smaller!

I’m not getting my hopes up, but it’s possible that rather than hunting for a job, I may be in the position of choosing a job. Bizarre. But I’m feeling pretty great about my prospects.

That was some fine Thai — I had the Tom Yum Kai (of course) and the duck ginger garlic basil (whatever); both were hot and tasty.
