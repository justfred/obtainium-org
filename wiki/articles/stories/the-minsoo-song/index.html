The Minsoo Song

From a letter to Diane, August 5, 1997.

I got a call from Minsoo — he’s been discharged. He wants to know what the status is of the corporation, and I’ve got no freaking idea. But I talked to Kip and he’s just started working on my file.

Kip is working on Minsoo’s papers — but I didn’t tell Minsoo that, because I don’t think he has a lot of respect for Kip.

Minsoo’s father is a diplomat, His uncle owns a laundrymat; He likes to wear a Darth Vader hat, Thinks he’s skinny but he’s really — not. — Minsoo song

That’s all I’ve got. He’s back in the world. The corporation still exists. Things are looking up.
