Felony Forest

Ken Little’s air freshener situation, named.

Ken Little’s car had a stack of Little Trees pine air fresheners hanging from the rearview mirror — many colors, piled up. This arrangement was known as “Felony Forest.”

Police call it that because a collection of air fresheners is a known strategy for masking the smell of drugs. Ken›?'s car did not contain drugs. It did contain an impressive commitment to pine scent.
