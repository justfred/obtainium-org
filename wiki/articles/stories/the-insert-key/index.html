The Insert Key

Hates the “insert” key. The only time I hit it is accidentally when I’m trying to hit “delete”; there’s no indicator whether it’s on or off; and it’s counter-intuitive (default behavior is insert; pressing it changes it to replace). Plus, my bet is the last time anyone used it intentionally was 1987.
