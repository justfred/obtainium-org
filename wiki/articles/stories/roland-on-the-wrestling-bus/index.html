Roland on the Wrestling Bus

The Webb School wrestling team. A road trip. A troglodyte assistant coach.

On a wrestling road trip, the assistant coach was driving the team van. He was chewing tobacco. He turned to Roland Dilley — who was from Montana, strong, quiet, good at wrestling — and said: “Hey Roland, you’re from Montana — ya ever f’d a cow?” He then went into the finer points of the activity at some length.

Roland endured this with the particular dignity of a large, silent person who has decided that responding is beneath him.

I tell this story mainly to document that the assistant coach said this. It seems worth having on record somewhere. The coach apparently felt that “being from Montana” was sufficient context for this line of inquiry.

Roland went on to have a life. I don’t know what happened to the coach. I suspect Roland’s life was better.
