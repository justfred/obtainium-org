Beer Bottle Calliope

May 9, 2012. Buzztime, Carlsbad.

The idea I’m brainstorming: a beer bottle calliope. I’ve figured out some of it, but I need to determine how to blow air across the bottles — I think it needs to be table-sized with a windcatcher on top. Need to figure out how to make it turn around.

What if it were upside-down? Then the pivot would be easier, as would the windcatcher.

Had an interesting idea for the CarouShell too — a beer bottle calliope of sorts.

The idea of a carousel-mounted, wind-powered beer bottle organ built inside an art car is exactly the kind of thing that sounds insane in an office and completely reasonable at 2 AM at Burning Man.
