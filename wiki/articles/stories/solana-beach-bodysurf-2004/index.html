Bodysurfing at Solana Beach

September 23, 2004. Solana Brewery.

I didn’t intend to stop and get a beer, but I drove by here and I just couldn’t help myself. Going to dinner tonight with Kip. Just got done with a great swim/bodysurf/bottom-gazing; warm clear water and lots of bat rays in evidence.

I haven’t felt like writing much lately; have been thinking of moving to LA and have been looking at maps and so forth. Ugh.

Need to get up to Pine Valley and clean out my car and do some thinking. Alone. Life is sort of interesting lately. Not sure even what I’ll be doing for work, or for where I’ll live, or for girlfriend, or what. At least the diet seems to be working out okay for me.
