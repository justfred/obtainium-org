Wateland Weekend

September 2019. Getting costumes ready.

“Going to Wateland Weekend and trying to get costumes ready.”

That’s the journal entry, dated September 5, 2019. Brief but certain.

Wateland Weekend is a water-themed regional event — the kind of gathering that exists in the Southern California Burning Man community’s orbit, close enough in spirit to the playa but more accessible, with fewer logistics. Water features. Probably a lake or river. Almost certainly excellent.

I hadn’t been to Burning Man since 2016. The years from 2017 onward had been years of Dylan — school schedules, early bedtimes, the general impossibility of doing a week in the desert when you have a small child and both parents are essential to the household running. The community events and regionals filled some of the gap.

The costumes required preparation. I didn’t elaborate on what they were. The journal entry was written at the moment of transition — contract ending in two weeks, Becke switching jobs, Dylan in kindergarten — and the costume prep was one item in a week with a lot of moving parts.

The event happened. The costumes were ready, or close enough. These things are usually close enough.
