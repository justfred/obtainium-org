The Cross-Country Pinz Dream

April 29, 2001. Pizza Port, Carlsbad.

It would be great to drive the Pinzgauer to the Treffen in Colorado — that’s about a week there — then between one and two weeks to get to Black Rock. Could even go up into Northern California if I wanted to. Then back, maybe spend a week and a half in the desert.

Wander around the West for a while at 50 miles per hour. If I’ve got a portable computer and camera, that’s really all the tech I need.

Leave the first part of August; drive to wherever it is in Colorado they’re having the Treffen. Then after: drop the Pinz in storage in Reno and fly back; fly up a week before Burning Man. That would be more practical and have me gone less, but I’d have to find someplace relatively decent to store it.

Or just drive the whole thing straight through. It’s a good dream.

I need to find a small military trailer for parts and gear so I can sleep in the back. Damn, that would be great — just wander around the West for a while at 50 miles per hour.
