Water Park Concussion

~2019. A water park in Southern California.

About 50 years old, at a water park with Dylan, age five. He and his mom were taking a break at the lazy river, so I went and rode one of the grown-up slides. On the way back through the park, a kid with an inner tube dashed around a corner into me and suddenly I was on my back — smacked my head against the concrete.

The first aid staff showed up right away. Got me to their small care center, checked for concussion, ice and a bandage. Professional and friendly. A fair amount of blood had to be hosed off. Head wounds are like that.

The kid did not stick around to see how things turned out. I don’t blame him — if I’d knocked a 200-pound adult onto his head at age ten I’d have been running too.

I was fine. Dylan never found out until later, which was the correct outcome. No sense ruining a water park day over something that healed inside a week.
