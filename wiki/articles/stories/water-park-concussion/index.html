Water Park Concussion

About 50 years old, at a water park with Dylan, age five. He and his mom were taking a break at the lazy river, so I went and rode one of the grown-up slides. On the way back, a kid with an inner tube dashed around me and suddenly I was on my back — smacked my head against the concrete.

The first aid staff showed up right away. Got me to their small care center, checked for concussion, ice and a bandage. Professional and friendly. A fair amount of blood had to be hosed off. Head wounds are like that.
