The Pinz: Burning Man Prep, 2000

Spring 2000. Fred is thinking about Burning Man and what to do with the Pinzgauer.

000508.2130 Home

Ready to take off my eyes. Also ready to get naked — I’d like to go to Black’s or somewhere I can strip off — also looking forward to Burning Man more and more each day. Trying to think of what to do — considered today making the Pinz into a loaf of bread, or a toaster. Time for me to go camping, too.
