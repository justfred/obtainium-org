Gorilla Ball Coconuts

Todd’s idea. Rum punch. A crate of coconuts for glasses. Tammy, Ray, and Liz showed up.

Todd’s idea. They made rum punch and got a crate of coconuts to use as glasses. “How much rum punch does a coconut hold? Who knows? How many have I had? Fuck if I know!”

Tammy Loos, Ray Tecotzky, and Liz Leonard showed up. Party at the Woodbury house in Altadena.

The coconuts had a slogan printed on them: “Bore hole, drink juice; crack nut, eat meat.”
