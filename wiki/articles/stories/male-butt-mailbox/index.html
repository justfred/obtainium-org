The Male Butt Mailbox

2003. Burning Man.

On Thursday afternoon some people pulled into our parking area with two motorhomes, set up and vanished, leaving a foam sign saying “Male Butt” — apparently supposed to be their mailbox. You can see pictures of it at the gallery.

So the next morning someone kidnapped it, and she and I drove it all around the city taking pictures with a disposable camera. Late that night we returned it and left the camera.

Sometimes the best art at Burning Man isn’t the art.
