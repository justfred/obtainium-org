Thomas: Contracts on Friday Night

~1990. Letter from Thomas (TK), undated. He’s in law school somewhere — “The Advocate is gone to print,” a campus publication he’s working on.

"Dear Fred,

I just spent a week suffering through: DOS, Compaq [(286)], HP [(LaserJet III)], Aldus [(PageMaker)], Windows [(3.0)]. (I saw lots of sunrises) (on my way home) The Advocate is gone to print :) so I have time (a little) again.

May be we can drive out after Xmas — Pick you up in Centro or something.

To answer your Q’s — School: It sucks (why ask) but I do have one bright point — Got Contracts for 3hrs Friday night — Chicks: They are running around this place in herds, but I have yet to bring down any stragglers (don’t send this letter to any women’s orgs.) — I did meet a blind girl, but she didn’t like my personality and ‘charm’."

He wrote his phone numbers at the bottom, labeled A and C — home and car — and circled them. He had all kinds of marginal notes running vertically along the right edge and upside down across the top.

Three hours of Contracts on Friday night. That’s law school. He saw lots of sunrises. He still had time for a road trip.
