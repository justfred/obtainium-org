Burning Man 2001: Planning the Drive

Summer 2001, Escondido. Fred›? is plotting his Burning Man logistics around the Cybertel chaos.

010628.1755 China Wokery San Marcos

I’ve been walking some, about every other day the past few days. I get about 7,000 steps or three and a half miles out of the standard circuit. I need to get myself into shape for Burning Man. Still not sure what I’ll do for that; would rather take the Pinzgauer›? and camp on the way but I really don’t want to deal with any breakdowns so I’d like to take it for service first.

I’d really rather take a bit longer vacation, maybe a week and a half if I can manage it.

010704.0932 Home

Yes, it looks like the last week of August is Burning Man. Wonder if I’ll have time to get the Pinz worked on by then. Wonder if I can tow the Pinz on a flat tow rig behind the Rover — or why the hell I would want to (would really rather drive it to Black Rock under its own power; maybe if I start driving it to work I’ll feel better about it).

I have yet to motivate to leave the house. Not sure quite what I’m going to do today but I guess I thought I could work on the database a little, then maybe buy and start building another plane or two. Because I can’t be bothered to do much anything else other than sit here journaling and watching reruns of cop shows on TV.

From an earlier spring entry (010429):

Leave the first part of August; drive to wherever it is in Colorado›? they’re having the Treffen. That’s about a week there, then between one and two weeks to get to Black Rock. Could even go up into northern California if I wanted to — see Rick and Helen? Then back, maybe spend a week and a half in the desert.

Thinking about how much I want to take the whole month: Me, someone who goes into work on Sunday knowing the company may be locked out of business on Tuesday by our vendor. I could really use a long drive in the desert.
