Tech Lead

April 14, 2008. Buzztime, Carlsbad.

Got a promotion last week — Tech Lead. Now I just need to live up to it.

I’m finding myself swamped at work; have been passing work to Son, which I feel like I should be doing, but I just haven’t had time, or I’m really slow at it. I’m better at understanding whole systems, I think, but I’m bad at remembering details, so I usually have to look them up.

I’ve started getting used to carrying the key drive around with me; I think the key is to leave it on my key ring so I can’t leave it behind. That and get a much bigger one, or get most of my stuff online so I don’t have to carry it with me. Photos are the biggest issue. That and the fact that the passwords file is there but not encrypted; really the whole drive should be encrypted.
