Dayquil: The Good Stuff

August 11, 2010. Flippin’ Pizza, Carlsbad.

I’ve had a cold the last few days, but it appears to have gone away now, or the medicine is working, or both. Have been snotty at night, especially Sunday, and coughing today.

The Dayquil that works is OTC, apparently — the other looks identical and is on the shelf, but apparently has a different main ingredient, because too many tweakers were buying the good stuff. Funny thing is the stuff that doesn’t work was $6, the stuff that does was $1.50 on special.

Better drink my beer. Pizza will be ready soon.
