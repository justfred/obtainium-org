Amy's South American Adventures

A family theory, maintained for years.

My sister Amy›? spent months in Peru and the South American rainforest. Multiple trips, extended stays, the kind of commitment to a place that most people don’t make.

We used to joke she was in the CIA and the “adventures” were just a cover story for black ops wet work.

This is funnier than it has any right to be because Amy is exactly the kind of person who could pull this off. She’s competent, adaptable, and not someone who explains herself more than necessary. The CIA cover story requires only that the person in question be capable and discreet. Amy is both.

The actual explanation is more interesting in some ways: she genuinely loves the rainforest and the work happening there. But the CIA theory has the advantage of being impossible to disprove. She’s never fully denied it.

I am not going to deny it either.
