Daily Grill Happy Hour

January 30, 2007. Daily Grill, Downtown LA.

I’m going to hang out here for a while and abuse their happy hour menu — all you can eat for $2.95 a plate, which isn’t great but the food is decent and I’m walking home — in the rain, most likely — so I might as well chill out for a while.

I’d really like a little time off, as a matter of fact. I want to work on my project, I want to go shopping for cheap camping equipment.

Some days my brain just runs over with ideas. Other days, it’s all I can do to stay awake.

There’s dinner. The chicken pot pie at $2.95 is a steal. I’m guessing $20 for dinner, which isn’t that great a bargain but what the hell.

The Daily Grill Bar has an internet connection. That works.
