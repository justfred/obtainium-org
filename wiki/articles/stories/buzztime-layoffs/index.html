Buzztime Layoffs

September 2, 2010. Karl Strauss Brewery, Carlsbad.

This morning, three more people were let go — which doesn’t seem like it should be earth-shattering, but it was the main focus of our meeting. I sort of wished they had talked more about the organizational changes.

The three lucky winners were: Mac, an artist who’s quirky but I don’t think his art skills are all that great; Teryll, who I don’t really know, did something to do with hardware, seemed like a fixture around the place and had a nice office near the front so he must have been of some importance; and Mary — to me this last one is very surprising. She’s been the voice of the players, if not for as long as I’ve been around, then for quite a while. She can be pretty negative sometimes but I thought she was a huge asset to the company. On the other hand, maybe she’ll be happier somewhere else.

I’ve got a bunch of stuff up for sale. The wired keyboard that came with the iPod I bought the other day is up to $25 with a few hours left on eBay. Buy on CL, sell on eBay seems to be a pretty good business plan.

I’m having a good Belgian Trippel. I don’t really need a lot of beer, but it’s nice to have a bit with lunch. Will see how my attitude is this afternoon.

It’s exciting to sell stuff on eBay because as the sale gets closer the price keeps bumping up and up.

Business lesson from football: you don’t need a touchdown every time. To stay in the game you just need a first down every four plays.

$25 for lunch is more than my budget, though I do get dinner out of it, too.

In July 2012 they let go Tim, Taryn, Cyndi, and thirteen sales reps. “Struggling to keep afloat,” I wrote. Our dev team kept getting smaller, the project list kept getting longer.
