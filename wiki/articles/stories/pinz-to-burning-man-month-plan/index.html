The Month-Long Pinz Trip I Planned

April 2001. Pizza Port, Carlsbad. After a few beers.

Would be weird to drive the Pinz›? all the way across the desert out to Colorado, then up to Burning Man. But I would like to take an entire month of vacation while I’m at it. That would be a real trip.

The plan, if it were a plan: leave early August. Drive to wherever they’re having the Treffen in Colorado. That’s about a week. Then one to two weeks to get to Black Rock. Could even go up into northern California. Then back, maybe spend a week and a half in the desert.

If I’ve got a portable computer and a camera, that’s really all the tech I need. As if that isn’t enough.

I could also do the Treffen, drop the Pinz in storage in Reno, fly back, then fly up a week before BM. More practical. But less of a thing.

I may need to find a small military trailer for parts and gear so I can sleep in the back. Damn, that would be great. Just wandering around the west at 50 miles per hour.

The Pinz did make it to Burning Man that year. The month-long plan did not happen.
