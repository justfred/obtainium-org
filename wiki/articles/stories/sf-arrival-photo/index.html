The SF Arrival Photo

1990. The Land Rover with the blue tarp still strapped on, Victorians behind. The far end of the Loneliest Road.

The SF arrival photo. Land Rover with the blue tarp still strapped on, Victorians behind us on the street. The other end of the Highway 50 trip — Anubis was somewhere in the middle.

(Susanne Meyer and me in front of the Land Rover, blue tarp, SF Victorians. We were about to leave for Houston, where she was going to stay with a roommate. The roommate fight happened before we arrived, the weather was awful, and we turned around and came home. I’m not a fan of flappy blue tarps.)
