Newton, Land Rover — Beautiful Anachronisms

April 18, 2000. Home, late.

Am I really back to using the Newton for a journal and a datebook/address book? Well, maybe I am. I know I like using it a lot more. The words do flow very nicely here even though the keyboard is a little clunky.

The thing is, most computers should really be designed a lot like this Newton. What a crying, pitiful shame. Even if the batteries go dead it doesn’t lose its mind, and the software all talks to itself, though not perfectly.

Newton, Land Rover — what other beautiful anachronisms? Buddhism?

I guess this saves as I go along — why the fuck don’t the usual computers?

This entry was written after Fred’s work laptop crashed and ate several pages of ranting. He’d gone to Dalton’s Roadhouse to write in the evening and came home resolved to return to the Newton as a journaling device. The Newton outlasted the laptop by several years. The Land Rover outlasted both.
