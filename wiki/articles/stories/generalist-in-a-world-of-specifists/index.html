Generalist in a World of Specifists

It’s hard being a generalist in a domain populated by specifists. I keep thinking of things that I can’t add to my resume. No, I don’t know Ember.js, but I do know how to weld, a little, or solder, somewhat better. Or program an Arduino. Or make rib eye sous vide. Or navigate Los Angeles on a Friday afternoon. Or find where the anemones are at the tidepools. How to make beer. All about the Big Texan in Amarillo. The capital of Burkina Faso. The different effects of aperture, exposure time, and ISO and how the golden ratio differs from the rule of thirds. How area code numbers work. And so forth.
