Fire Spinning and Butter Dog

June 8, 2004.

Things I’m thinking about, in no particular order other than that in which they come to mind:

  • CVS. Been meaning to learn this, I should really know it inside and out because it seems like the sort of thing I’m into.

  • Fire spinning. I got a wooden staff and I’ve been practicing with it.

  • Two words: butter dog.

  • Rangering (as opposed to ‘Rodgering’ — thanks Scix). Think I’m going to do it.

  • TOA construction. Enjoyed it the other day; want to ingratiate myself with those people 'cause I think they’re marvelous.

  • BMAN camp. Thermal shock? On my own? Shangrila?

  • Oh, right, work. I’m being paid to be here, aren’t I? And not just to watch the clock tick away till I can stop fucking around and go home.

  • Books. I keep acquiring more and more books; can’t resist. Have been cataloging them in Booxter, would love a way to sync that with the palm so what happened the other day wouldn’t: rebought a book I already own.

  • Palm. I’ve been carrying it around, and syncing it… and rarely using it. I’ve got the keyboard and cable with me now, but it’s probably just dead weight I’m schlepping; I do all my work on the Mac and I’m spoiled; I don’t always carry the palm and I really don’t ever trust it. Maybe someday technology will improve but for now it still is a hack.
