Fire Poi at the Wedding

~2006. Our wedding ceremony.

I have friends who are fire performers. They did the closing show at our wedding using fire poi and fire staffs. About halfway through the performance, the lead performer hit herself with a wet poi, and her pants caught fire.

Fortunately they had a good safety person there with a wet towel. The fire went out quickly. No injuries.

It gave our parents’ friends something to talk about for the rest of the reception, and probably for years afterward.

I’m told this is not the typical outcome at weddings with fire performers. I maintain it made the event more memorable.
