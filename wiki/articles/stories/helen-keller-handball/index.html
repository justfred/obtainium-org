The Helen Keller Invitational

El Centro›?. First time driving a car.

The first day I drove the first car I ever drove, I transported an open container of Budweiser — a tapped keg — to a handball tournament.

The tournament was called the “First Annual Helen Keller Invitational.” The power went out.

I’m not going to explain the Helen Keller reference, because the tournament’s name was the joke and the tournament’s name is self-explanatory. The power went out because El Centro is in a desert and desert power infrastructure in the 1970s and 80s was what it was.

I was driving the car. This was the beginning of a long career of driving vehicles of various types, quality levels, and road-fitness. The keg was in the back. The tournament was waiting. The power was not yet out.

When I tell this story to explain how I became a driver, it’s mostly because “I drove a keg of Budweiser to a handball tournament named after Helen Keller on my first day behind the wheel” is the kind of origin story that sets appropriate expectations for everything that came after.
