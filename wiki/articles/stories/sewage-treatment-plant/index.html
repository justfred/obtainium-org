The Sewage Treatment Plant

My Mom took us to the grand opening of the local sewage treatment plant. It was actually interesting to see how sewage gets turned back into drinkable water — lots of holding ponds and aerators and so forth. And they were glad to show us around since we were the first (only?) guests to show up that day.

Pretty stinky, even compared to nearby cattle feedlots.
