The Grand Opening of the Sewage Treatment Plant

El Centro›?, Imperial Valley, early 1970s.

My mom took us to the grand opening of the local sewage treatment plant.

It was actually interesting. You get to see how sewage gets turned back into drinkable water: lots of holding ponds, aerators, machinery. The staff was glad to show us around, since we were apparently the first — possibly only — guests to show up that day.

Pretty stinky, even compared to the nearby cattle feedlots.

This is not a metaphor. She just thought it was educational. It was.
