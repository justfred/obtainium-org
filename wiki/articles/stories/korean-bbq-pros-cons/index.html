Korean BBQ: Pros and Cons

Pro: Meat. More meat. Even more meat.

Con: Is there such a thing as too much meat? I would never have believed it.

Pro: Little bowls of vegetables and pickled stuff.

Con: What the hell is it? Who knows? My Korean friend always says “I don’t know the word in English. It’s good, though.”

Pro: It’s all you can eat.

Con: It’s not all you care to eat, it’s all you can eat. Protein coma.

Pro: Staff are always helpful.

Con: Staff rarely speak English, can sometimes be a bit “too” helpful. I know when to turn my bulgogi, thanks.

Pro: It’s cheap.

Pro/Con: It’s a lot better if you go with a group of friends.
