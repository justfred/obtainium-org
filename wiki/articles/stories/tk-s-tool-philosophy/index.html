TK's Tool Philosophy

The one-tool thought experiment. Fred and TK disagree in the most predictable way possible.

Fred and Thomas Kendall had a discussion: if you could have only one tool, what would it be? Fred said a vice grip — you could do nearly anything with it. TK said: “A really large hammer.”
