The New Mac Server ('Yikes')

June 26, 2003. Penny Lane Pub, San Marcos.

Not a bad day today — I got the new Mac server (“Yikes” or “Yoiks”) up and running, and even managed to bump the speed up to 400MHz (we’ll see how that works out). And I got a DVD-RAM drive and got Plaid’s stuff downloaded. Wonder if she’ll want to buy the drive; otherwise it doesn’t hurt me to have a spare DVD drive I suppose, especially if it’ll read anything you throw at it.

D’oh, battery warning. Since I’d rather not lose everything I suppose I’ll try writing in the old book for a while.
