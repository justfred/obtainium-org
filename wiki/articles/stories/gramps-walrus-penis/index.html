Gramp's Walrus Penis

When Gramp›? and Gram›? moved from their five-bedroom house to a care facility.

When my grandparents moved from their two-story five-bedroom house to a residential care facility, the kids and grandkids descended to deal with all their stuff. At some point my Dad got a call from Gramp.

“In the bedroom closet there’s a walrus penis.”

Gramp was a paleontologist — among many other hobbies — and he had a petrified walrus penis, or penis bone, or whatever the technical term is, about two feet long, looking like a stone dildo. For some reason he hadn’t left it out on the display shelf with the other bones and artifacts.

My parents got to keep it. My dad has it on one of their shelves somewhere. I got a skull that Gramp had preserved when he was in medical school, which I think is the better deal aesthetically, but to each his own.

Gramp was a man of remarkable range.
