New Orleans, Election Week 2000

November 2000. Fred›? is on a work trip to New Orleans›? and Pittsburgh during the week of the Bush-Gore election that wouldn’t end.

001110J — Red Bull Inn, Pittsburgh

It’s been a long and eventful week. This is the week of the presidential election that would not end. And I’ve been spending it in New Orleans and (mostly) Pittsburgh. Tired of checking CNN to see more mixed news — Bush is still ahead but Gore will be disputing the results; in any case the election will be nondecisive for a long time, and even after it’s settled will be no great power for the winner. Truly the results an anarchist would love.

The table next to me is filled with nuns, which didn’t strike me as all that strange until I realised — it’s Friday. And this is a steak place. Am curious if they’re all having the lobster, which although it’s not kosher must be okay.

“Adam and Eve on a raft — wreck 'em!”

001112J — Cafe Beignet, Decatur Street, New Orleans

Have had a fairly relaxing day, once I got off the plane. Fairly convenient to stay at the airport hotel before leaving early the next morning. Got about four hours sleep, then a couple hours on the plane.

Woman behind the counter is coughing — probably another case of walking Ebola. And of course I’m getting powdered sugar all over myself. Have had a bit of full stomach most of the day, I guess that’s called indigestion.

(Later; Crescent City Brewery)

Saw an interesting surrealist artist who caught my eye. Almost would like to own one but at $4300 a bit hard to justify. Perhaps if I were an independently wealthy software developer.

These are more delicate little oysters than the ones I had the other night, but they’re a fine little protein burst.
