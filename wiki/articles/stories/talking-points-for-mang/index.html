Talking Points for Mang

June 2001. Fred›? is preparing for a showdown meeting with Mangiarelli and Rick Schmidt over his Cybertel stock.

010611.2202 Home

So I guess tomorrow I get to meet with Rick Schmidt and Mangiarelli. Or maybe Wednesday, I can’t be sure. But it’s likely it won’t be fun and I’ll just have to sit there and refuse to negotiate down at all.

Contract/Stock Issues

  • I have my stock now. Although it is legally mine, I’m sure I would not be able to sell it without a legal battle.
  • If you don’t want Telenomics stock, why should I want it?
  • You never gave me a written release of the bogus fraud charges.
  • Having to worry about this is taking away from my productivity.
  • I just plain can’t trust you.

Personal Issues (My Job)

  • There’s too much work for a person to do; and you’re coming up with more every day.
  • I’ve been working weekends to get the bills out because there are so many mistakes in the database; new mistakes seem to be added every day.
  • I need a vacation; I intend to take a vacation in August.

Cybertel Issues (the Company)

  • Your half-time MIS person is not enough.
  • The database is too complex for the application.
  • This was supposed to be a technology company; and yet at the moment you only have 1½ people out of 20 who know fuck all about technology.
  • How are you ever going to build a network with the kind of staff you’ve got?
  • You just fired half the staff in Vista›?.
  • When a company fires its sales staff, it’s a good sign that it’s going down the toilet.
