Gordon Biersch After The Matrix

June 21, 2003. Gordon Biersch, Mountain View.

Played two rounds of disc golf this morning, enjoyed the hell out of myself and actually think I’m getting a little better for having a bit of practice.

Then I saw The Matrix, which (I sound like Kip›?) made no sense whatsoever; but the effects were okay. I’d have liked it a bit better if most of the movie weren’t dumb fake fight scenes.

The “entertainment” here is… baseball, baseball, and… (car commercials)… more fucking baseball. What a damned snooze.

Is it really that difficult to make one damned interface and stick to it? Across platforms? It’s really not like any of them end up with any real advantages over the others. Especially bad with palmtops and phones. Shit — it could just be USB and they could do everything they needed! Damned idjits.

Not sure why I so like to do the journalling — gives me something to do, be nerdy — but at the same time I’m interacting with people a little. Beats reading a book.
