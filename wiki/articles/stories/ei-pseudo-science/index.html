EI Is a Load of Horseshit

July 23, 2010.

10:35. Apparently I’m supposed to be recording my emotions so I can evaluate them. At the moment I’m feeling depressed. I got the results from the Emotional Intelligence assessment this morning. I don’t know if this process is supposed to be helping anyone, but it certainly isn’t helping me — mainly it’s made me feel like crap for a month, every time I have to deal with it.

“EI” is apparently a pseudo-science, and from what I’ve read I don’t put any value to it. The one page of the 29-page report I got that was any use was the freeform negative feedback — and that was all mixed messages.

I don’t want to meet with Katy and Shannon to discuss it, because they’ve both shown that they’re clueless about what’s going on here. Maybe Steve? Chad? Would have some concept.

To me the problem is that our process is entirely screwed up to the point that we can’t get anything done well or effectively. Sure, this makes me react emotionally, but that isn’t the problem that’s causing this.

13:44. This process doesn’t seem productive, just cruel. Like making me lie down blindfolded and encouraging my coworkers to kick me. I don’t believe meeting to talk about it could be useful except to let Shannon check a box saying it had been done. Best to try to put it behind me if I can.

At least I’ve been working on bugs lately which gives me something to do. If I really put any thought into it I’d realize that most of the people I work with are hopelessly stupid and incompetent.

14:17. They refuse to recognize that this is a problem with process, not with personnel.
