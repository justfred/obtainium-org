Getting COVID

January 2022.

I got COVID in January 2022. Called in sick to work but did some work anyway, because the alternative was lying in bed feeling guilty about the work. Symptoms: cough, runny nose, muscle aches. First day I missed my Apple Watch movement numbers in six months. Slept till 2pm.

The test was easy — quick, no wait. Positive result. This was Omicron, mostly, by that point: highly transmissible, generally less severe than earlier variants. I got it, dealt with it, recovered over several days.

The most notable thing about having COVID in January 2022 was how unremarkable it was as an experience compared to what we’d all been afraid of in 2020. Which is not the same as saying COVID isn’t serious. It is saying that by early 2022, being vaccinated and getting a mild case felt like exactly what it was: an illness you dealt with and recovered from.

I went back to normal movement ring counts the following week. The Apple Watch was satisfied. I was satisfied. We moved on.
