Guadalupe Canyon Gathering

March 2008. Guadalupe Canyon, Baja›?.

Spent this weekend in Guadalupe Canyon, at a burner gathering — Fuente Eterno. It was fine, though I did get pulled over for a stop sign in Mexicali, and ran out of gas as I passed through the border. But it could have been worse — I ran through a bunch of mud on the dry lake, but didn’t get stuck, and didn’t have to spend a lot of time cleaning up our big camping mess; didn’t get busted for contraband or break anything in the vehicle on the wicked dirt road.

Have a few mosquito bites to show for it and I feel like I gained five pounds eating lunch at Camacho’s.

Got a haircut tonight, finally — didn’t get it too short but it does feel a lot lighter.
