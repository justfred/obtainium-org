iPhone Disappointment

January 15, 2007. Good Microbrewery, Los Angeles›?.

I’m thinking I’m disappointed with the iPhone — except for the fact that it basically paid for this laptop — due to the fact that it isn’t an ultraportable OSX machine. A few months till we actually find out I suppose, but it’s seeming as if it isn’t aimed at developers, which really spoils everything I might use it for.

It’s a little annoying to have an internet connection that works all the time — like having a cellphone that always has reception.

I’m really bored with the internet. I can easily find porn that will get me off, I guess, and the usual array of 50-some websites I look at every day; but there isn’t that much more than that. Beats the library when it comes to research of course, but.

Wonder if I could be an Apple Genius. I guess I could sell Macs for a while, probably not a lot of money in that, but maybe a better job in the offing.

The women at the next table are a little goofy. Whenever I think about dating and all that crap, I’m happier that I’m with Becke.

Two beers and some steak for me tonight, and tomatoes instead of potatoes.
