The iBook Arrives, 2001

June 2001. Fred has been waiting on a new iBook order, living in Escondido, riding out the Telenomics/Cybertel disaster.

010601.1937 Home

And the first journal entry on the new iBook. Damned fine machine I must say even though I haven’t had much of a chance to use it; and of course moving everything to OSX has been a bit of a challenge.

This fucking computer rocks. In comparison to the other computers I bought the other day (Telenomics’) I would far rather be using this. Airport is really cool; I’m online now, tho I haven’t needed to be. That’s why I’m trying to get all my email straightened out.

I had the best damn flying day I ever had, this afternoon. Lots of good steady wind, not a lot of other planes trying to knock me down, my plane is lighter and a lot better behaved.

Only things I’ve found so far about the iBook (and OSX) that are a little annoying are the battery life when sleeping and the wake up from sleep — both of which may be related to OSX and may be fixed soon in their frequent updates. Also will take a little bit of getting used to this keyboard; I like it but it catches my fingers.

I could sit here and work on perl code, I guess. I like to think of myself as sort of a modern-day Hemmingway. I suppose if I were actually writing stories instead of this I’d qualify.

Once again I wonder if anyone will ever read this journal; I still can’t be sure it won’t be lost irretrievable, tomorrow.
