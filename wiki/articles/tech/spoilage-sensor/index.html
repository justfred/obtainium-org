The Spoilage Sensor

Why don’t we have spoilage sensors on our food yet? Especially milk and meat. I’m sure they give off some sort of chemically traceable gas — why not a sticker that changes color in the presence of a specified concentration? Not that I’m too lazy to just smell it, but there are times when you can’t really tell. Failing that, why not a standard readable sticker listing how long it may be kept and still be edible?
