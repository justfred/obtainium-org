Telluride and Ouray

June 2023 · Age 9

About midway through the Colorado›? trip, we spent time in two mountain towns.

In Ouray›? we stayed in a hotel with a big pool, a free breakfast, and hot tubs right in the building — Dylan approved.

In Telluride›?, a mountain town with a park in the middle of it and a river running nearby, we rode the free gondola up to the top of the mountain — Dylan loved it — and walked around town, stopping to talk to people and their dogs. Near the park there was a tiny creek, and Dylan found some sticks and leaves and floated them in it. Dylan was nine years old and it was a mountain creek. Down at the river, dogs were chasing balls their owners had thrown in and swimming back to shore. Dylan watched them do this for a while.
