The Hike

November 2019 · Dora, age 11

When you were five, Papa and Dora went for a long hike. Two hours — more walking than either of them had done in a long time. Dora was eleven years old by then, which is getting up there for a dog. Papa wasn’t young either. But they both made it, and neither of them complained.
