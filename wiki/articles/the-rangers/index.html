The Black Rock Rangers

January 1, 2005

Black Rock City — mid-2000s to present.

“We’re not cops, we’re not your mom.”

The Black Rock Rangers are a volunteer peacekeeping organization at Burning Man — the people in khaki who show up when something’s wrong. Not law enforcement, not medical. Something closer to community elders with radios. The philosophy is harm reduction and curiosity.

My playa name is Ranger Mushroom. It was acquired at someone else’s initiative, as playa names tend to be. The Rangers had a venerable OG named Badger; the “Badger Badger Badger (Mushroom Mushroom)” meme was circulating when I joined. The name was partly imposed to harass Badger — standard tradition. But I’d also been talking on that shift about mushrooms: edible, deadly, or trippy. The Rangers thought that framed the role well. See: Ranger Mushroom

My primary role is Operator — sitting in a shack at Ranger Outpost Berlin, listening to the radio, transcribing incidents. A six-hour shift covers a cross-section of what Black Rock City actually is. In 2022 I also did a Green Dot shift: emotional support, quietly available, no fixing. See: The Operator Shift
