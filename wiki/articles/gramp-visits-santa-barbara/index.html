Gramp, and the Drive to Santa Barbara

Fall 2001. Fred is worried about Gramp, who is old and ill, and keeps thinking he should make the drive up to visit.

010926.2017 Home

I need to visit Gramp. And I guess maybe I should do that this weekend, trying to think what else I have to do. No, that’d probably be a good idea. I hope he doesn’t kill himself — by stopping his dialysis I suppose — but I don’t really think there’s all that much I could do about it. But I would like to see him again.

011103J — Friday’s, Oxnard

I’m on my way up to Santa Barbara; I wanted to see Gramp. I got it into my head that I wanted to go to Outback and get some steak but it was too crowded and I ended up here.

I’m feeling very tired at the moment. I’m also feeling lonely and frustrated with everything.

I suppose I should get in to work sometime. I really should have taken this weekend to go up to see Gramp, won’t be able to do that again soon and he may not be there all that long. I know Gram is functionally gone.
