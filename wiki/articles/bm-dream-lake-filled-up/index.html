Burning Man Dream: The Lake Filled Up

This morning I had another Burning Man dream. The dry lake had filled up, and the event had been moved to the shore; we were housed in abandoned dormitories (that I’ve seen in other dreams). Bruce (Manboy/Techno) was there, and had programs printed up on what looked like currency — “If you don’t like the show, I will clean your house.” Also remember trying to explain it to cute newbie girls.

(I now realise that I’ve seen the shore in another dream, too, except it was in Hawaii and there were waves.)

Source: Journal, January 2003
