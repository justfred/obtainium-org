Ski Trip: Utah and Zion

January 1, 1990

A ski trip to Utah with Mom, Dad, and Thomas Kendall. Fred falls on ice. The Jeep smells like salad.

A ski trip to Utah with Mom and Dad and Thomas Kendall. I fell on the ice and scraped up my face. The later shots were at Zion. On the way we stopped for groceries and broke a bottle of salad dressing in the back of the Jeep. The Jeep smelled like salad from that point on.
