  • Ancient Mariner

    A Jeff "Beachbum" Berry creation — a split of Jamaican and Demerara rum spiced with allspice dram, balanced by lime and grapefruit.

  • Angostura Colada

  • Banana Foster

    A dessert-style shot — cinnamon whisky, banana liqueur, and whipped-cream vodka, evoking bananas Foster.

  • Banana Toast Crunch

    A creamy dessert drink — Fireball and RumChata over banana, topped with club soda. The source gives no quantities, so none are invented below.

  • Corn and Oil

    A Barbadian classic Fred calls "a fantastic quick drink" — Barbados rum, falernum, and Angostura, stirred.

  • Cthulhu's Curse

    A spiced-rum tiki sketch with pineapple bitters as the signature note. The source lists only ingredients — no quantities — so none are invented below. Flagged as terse.

  • Daiquiri

    The classic Cuban daiquiri — white rum, lime, and simple syrup, shaken and served up. The benchmark sour.

  • Dark and Stormy

    The Bermudian classic — dark rum floated over ginger beer. Fred adds a dash of bitters and optional lime.

  • Don Ho Suck 'Em Up Mai Tai

    A juice-forward Mai Tai variant with a dark Jamaican / light rum split, pineapple and orange rounding out the citrus.

  • El Presidente

    A pre-Prohibition Cuban classic — white rum, dry vermouth, curaçao, and a touch of grenadine, stirred and served up like a rum martini.

  • Hawaiian Eye

    A falernum-spiced tiki sour with a light/dark rum split (Fred calls for Bacardi 8 as the dark).

  • Honi Honi

    Basically a Mai Tai using the original Trader Vic's formulation but substituting bourbon for the rum. "Honi Honi" supposedly means "Kiss kiss" in Hawaiian. Fred had this on…

  • Jungle Bird

    A 1970s Malaysian tiki classic that brought Campari into the tiki canon — bittersweet against dark rum and pineapple.

  • Mai Tai

    The Trader Vic classic — the cornerstone tiki cocktail. Fred's build leans on a split of Jamaican and dark rum with orgeat (or falernum) and orange curaçao.

  • Miniature Hurricane

    A pared-down Hurricane — dark Jamaican rum with passion fruit and lemon. Fred used a rough mix of Appleton Special, Appleton 12, and Wray for the rum.

  • Mr Bali Hai

    Named for the San Diego tiki landmark — a coffee-brandy tiki sour over a light/dark Jamaican rum split. "Cheers friends!"

  • Navy Grog

  • NSAID

    Not Fred's own — picked up somewhere on the web. A Painkiller riff swapping eggnog mix in for the coconut cream. The name continues the painkiller/anti-inflammatory pun.

  • Orange Blossom

    An Ed Hamilton recipe — pimento (allspice) dram and overproof Hamilton 86 demerara rum with orange juice.

  • Painkillah (Painkiller)

    The British Virgin Islands classic, traditionally made with Pusser's rum. Creamy, pineapple-forward, and dangerously easy. (Fred's note spells it "Painkillah.")

  • Queens Park Swizzle

    A classic Trinidadian rum swizzle — demerara rum, lime, and mint built over crushed ice and swizzled until the glass frosts, finished with a float of Angostura bitters.

  • Scorpion

    A classic Trader Vic communal tiki drink — rum and brandy with citrus and orgeat, blended with crushed ice.

  • Suffering Bastard

    A WWII-era hangover cure from Cairo's Shepheard's Hotel. Fred's note lists only the spirits and balance — no quantities given, so none are invented below.

  • Tapa Punch

    A rum punch with a peach brandy accent and a dark/light rum split. (Note title runs as "Tap a Punch" in the source — likely Tapa Punch.)
