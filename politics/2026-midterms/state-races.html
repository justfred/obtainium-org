2026 State Races — Governors, Election Officials, Legislatures, Courts

Snapshot: 2026-10-24. Election: Tue Nov 3.

Here’s why this matters

The people elected to these offices will run the 2028 presidential election, draw the next congressional maps, and file most of the lawsuits against the federal government.

  • Governors sign or veto maps and election laws, appoint replacements when a Senate seat opens (SC, FL and OH all have appointed senators on this ballot), and in most states certify presidential electors.
  • Secretaries of state administer and certify elections. Several 2026 candidates have denied past election results.
  • Attorneys general lead the multistate lawsuits that act as the main legal check on the administration.
  • Legislatures draw maps, which mid-decade redistricting has made a live issue every cycle. Flipping a single chamber ends one-party control of a state.
  • State supreme courts decided this year’s map fights and rule on abortion and on contested elections.
  • Turnout drops further down the ballot, so these races are where targeted turnout counts most.
