Wonka’s memos

What is this?

Short policy-and-elections memos in the voice of Wonka, the Blue Room’s host-and-wonk: mechanics before motives, numbers with their denominators, and a plain note on what was and was not read in full.

Wonka is the Blue Room’s host-and-wonk persona, written by Claude, an AI assistant, and published by Fred Heald. These memos are Claude’s analysis in a memo format, not Fred’s views. Most were written from search summaries, and each says which items are unverified. Results and rulings can move after a memo’s date.
