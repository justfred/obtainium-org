Fact check: “Something I find funny about you people who ‘think’ you won...”

Verdict at a glance: Mostly overstated or reversed; two bullets are labels, not claims.

What is this about?

A claim-by-claim check of a five-bullet image meme aimed at Democratic voters after the 2026 midterms, ending “cutting off your nose to spite your face.” Original poster and date unknown.

Written by Claude, an AI assistant, and published by Fred Heald. It is Claude’s analysis, not Fred’s views. Checked 2026-11-07 from search summaries only (no pages read in full).

One-sentence overview

Two bullets start from something real (a floated $5,000 check; record-low murder rates), but neither supports the accusation attached to it; the tax-cut bullet reverses what Democrats mostly propose; and the other two are labels, not claims.

The meme (transcribed)

Headline: “SOMETHING I FIND FUNNY ABOUT YOU PEOPLE WHO ‘THINK’ YOU WON......” Each bullet ends “just to ‘beat Trump’”:

  1. You lost a $5,000 check for every American adult.
  2. You’d give up the lowest violent crime rate in 100 years by trying to defund ICE/police.
  3. You will support the transworship, hurting women, and mutilating children agenda.
  4. You risk losing tax cuts for tips, overtime, Social Security, secure borders, and energy dominance.
  5. You take illegals, radicals, terrorists, sharia law, trans activists, and socialists/communists over Americans.

Closer: “This is the definition of cutting off your nose to spite your face.”

Claim by claim

# Claim Verdict Notes
1 Democratic voters “lost” a $5,000 check for every adult Unsupported The $5,000 “DOGE dividend” was an idea (investor James Fishback’s; Musk said he’d “check with the president”). It would have needed Congress, depended on DOGE hitting a $2 trillion savings goal, and was limited to net income-tax payers. Reports say GOP lawmakers, including Speaker Johnson, kept batting it down. No law, no vote, and I found nothing showing Democrats blocked it. See also the Gizmodo entry (8) on DOGE’s savings record.
2a “Lowest violent crime rate in 100 years” Partly For murder, supported with a caveat: Jeff Asher told NPR the U.S. almost certainly had its lowest murder rate on record in 2025 (FBI data go back to 1960; he said a few 1950s years were slightly lower; some CDC series go back further). A figure of 4.1 per 100,000 is reported secondhand, unconfirmed. For violent crime as a whole, “100 years” is not established; FBI series start in 1960. Asher also says it’s “not a solved problem” (13,000-14,000 murders).
2b Democrats are “trying to defund ICE/police” Overstated Police: I found no 2026 Democratic leadership push to defund police. ICE: a real split. The DNC passed both a “reform” and an “abolish ICE” resolution (Aug 2026); Sen. Markey and Rep. Thanedar called for abolition; centrists and Schumer pushed narrower limits (warrants, ending patrols). A GOP source says 206 Democrats voted to defund DHS in January; that is a partisan framing of a funding vote, and some Democrats voted the other way.
2c Defunding ICE would cost the crime decline Unsupported Implied cause, no mechanism or evidence. The decline is national and runs across cities with very different policing and ICE postures. I did not check when it began; the sources describe 2025 as the record year and early 2026 as lower still.
3 Support “transworship... hurting women, mutilating children” Value (not checkable) “Transworship” is not a defined policy. “Mutilating children” is a moral label for gender-affirming care for minors; Democratic positions on it vary and I did not research them here. The “just to beat Trump” frame assigns a motive to voters.
4a Voting this way risks tips and overtime tax cuts Reversed The deductions are real (OB3) and are set to expire after tax year 2028. The searches found Democrats mostly want to expand or make them permanent (one bill would extend overtime to union and airline workers; Rep. Susie Lee backs removing the 2028 expiry). They did oppose the overall bill. A change in who holds the House can’t repeal a law without the Senate and President.
4b “...Social Security” Overstated The tip deduction doesn’t reduce Social Security or Medicare payroll taxes. OB3 has a temporary senior deduction; no one is shown trying to repeal it.
4c “...secure borders, energy dominance” Not a tax cut Policy goals, not tax provisions. Nothing found shows a vote or bill that removes them.
5 Taking “illegals, radicals, terrorists, sharia law, trans activists, socialists/communists” over Americans Value / insult A list of labels. “Sharia law” and “terrorists” name no policy or person; I know of no Democratic proposal for either, but did not search it.

Shape (for the log)

  • Second-person accusation (“you people”) plus scare quotes on “THINK” to open.
  • Refrain: every bullet ends “just to ‘beat Trump’,” which turns every position into spite.
  • Motive attribution to a whole group of voters, not to a named person.
  • Real kernel, wrong link: bullets 1 and 2 start from a real event (a floated check; low murder rates) and attach a cause that isn’t shown.
  • A mismatch on bullet 4: it warns of losing a benefit the other side mostly wants to keep or expand.
  • Closing idiom doing the argument’s work. Flag colors, red keywords, no sources.
  • Same bones as memes 5 to 7 (headline, contrast, closing insult); the direction is reversed.

What would change the read

  • A dated, attributable source for the meme.
  • A bill or vote showing Democrats opposing the tip or overtime deductions (none found), or showing an actual police-defunding proposal in 2026.
  • The FBI’s final 2025 report to confirm the murder-rate figure.
